Tests and fixtures the range makes false: `tests/src/core/constants.test.ts` (the upper bound: below 8), `tests/src/core/compilers.test.ts` snapshots and rows (regenerate through the suite's snapshot update only for the rows that carry the range or the bridge; read each diff), `tests/src/bin/CLI.test.ts:1172, :1206, :1223, :1258, :1276, :1525`, `tests/src/bin/helpers.test.ts:344`, `tests/src/core/fixtures/app-only-toolchain.txt:14`, `setup-false-manifest.txt:72`, `source-manifest.txt:72`; derive the rest by running the suite.
