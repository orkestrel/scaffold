# The full break from TypeScript 6.0.3 to 7.0.2 — reconciliation and plan

Ruled by the Orchestrator on 2026-09-05 over the fleet sweep (`sweep-distillate.md`), the research lanes (`research-{a,b,c}-report.md`, `propagation-report.md`), the design lanes (`design-subjective.md`, `design-objective.md`; Sol dark, both on Opus, recorded), and the measurements (`orchestrator-measurements.md`). The owner's instruction: no `@typescript/typescript6`, no peer widening, every package on 7, no backwards compatibility.

The one compiler that is not ours to move: `@microsoft/api-extractor` 7.59.0 pins `typescript` 5.9.3 as its own dependency and analyses with it. Our declared pins are 7-only; the install graph carries that 5.9.3 under api-extractor, as it does today. The alternative, `rolldown-plugin-dts`'s `tsgo` generator, is a new dependency and fixes `rootDir` to the tsconfig's directory, which refuses every `configs/src/tsconfig.*.json`.

## Decisions only the owner can take

1. **Vue.** A 7-only fleet cannot type-check `.vue` files at all until Volar reaches TypeScript 7.1's API: not the templates, and not the `<script setup lang="ts">` bodies. Accept that loss for generated `app/browser` workspaces (no fleet package selects that environment), or hold `app/browser` generation until Volar supports 7.
2. **api-extractor's engine.** Keep api-extractor with its bundled 5.9.3 for the rollup, or refuse any 5.x compiler in the install graph and take `rolldown-plugin-dts` (a new dependency the plan would add at the owner's request, plus a root-level tsconfig per environment or an upstream change).
3. **The preview surface.** probe's type stage, the generated proof, and the test readers all move onto `typescript/unstable/*`, which Microsoft replaces at 7.1. That is one scaffold release plus one fleet visit again when 7.1 ships.

## Exit criterion

No manifest in scaffold or the fleet declares `@typescript/typescript6`, `vite-plugin-dts`, or `vue-tsc`, and every `typescript` range reads `^7.0.2`; no source, test, config, or script imports the bridge or a compiler from the bare `typescript` specifier; probe answers a claim under 7.0.2 with a receipt naming `typescript@7.0.2` and the caller's project digest; every `src`-publishing package rebuilds its declarations through `tsc` 7 and api-extractor with green gates, has diffed its `dist/` against the published tarball, and the movers have published in layer order; the guides, the rule files, and `ROADMAP.md` carry the Vue loss, its trigger, and the retired rows; the fleet-wide proof command passes.

## Risks carried

The preview API's 7.1 replacement; api-extractor's engine over `tsc` 7's emit on faces not yet measured; `tsc` 7 refusing declaration emit past a type error a 6.x build tolerated; a per-file native client under Vitest's parallelism on a small host; `stripTypeScriptTypes`'s Stability 1 output drifting across Node patches; a full-fleet publish wave under the serial-upload rules.
