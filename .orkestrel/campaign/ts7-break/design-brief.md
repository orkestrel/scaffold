## Decisions the plan must take, each with its rationale and its cost

1. **Probe's type stage.** The `--api` sync surface (measured: overlay filesystem, scoped project, 60 ms, `Diagnostic {fileName, pos, end, code, category, text}`; preview, replaced at 7.1) versus the language server (`tsc --lsp --stdio`; protocol-stable; 2 s to the first answer; project selection implicit; the same `@orkestrel/lsp` client the lint stage already drives). Which one, what `Toolchain`, `Project`, and the receipt digest read under it, what the published declarations import types from once `@typescript/typescript6` is gone, and what the `typescript` peer becomes (`^7.0.2`; optional or required; what a workspace that installs no `typescript` gets).
2. **Declarations.** `tsc` 7 emit plus api-extractor's own engine (measured; drops `vite-plugin-dts`/`unplugin-dts`; keeps api-extractor, whose bundled 5.9.3 is its own engine and not a dependency of ours on 6) versus `rolldown-plugin-dts` (a new dependency; the `rootDir` limit). Which one, how the `build:src:*` scripts and the `configs/src/vite.*.config.ts` seeds change, and whether every fleet package's `dist/` moves (the `import type` diff) and therefore bumps.
3. **The AST-shaped rules.** The vendored policy sweep's AST rules move to the oxlint `policy` plugin (`ROADMAP.md` already names this as a law-conformance repair): which rules those are, what the plugin's ESTree surface gives them, and what stays text-shaped in `tests/setupPolicy.ts` with no compiler at all. The same question for `tests/guides.test.ts`'s helper lifting, `tests/src/core/templates.test.ts`'s parsing, and `lsp/tests/setupConformance.ts`'s import walk: the `--api` surface over a scratch project, the oxlint plugin, a text scan, or `oxc-parser` (a new dependency the owner must request).
4. **Type-checking in tests.** The generated distribution proof (`createProgram` + checker over installed declarations) and `database/tests/setupServer.ts` (programs, exports, fence checks): the `--api` surface (`Program`, `Checker.getExportsOfModule`, per-kind diagnostics) versus spawning `tsc --noEmit -p` over a scratch project. Which one per site.
5. **Fences.** `stripTypeScriptTypes({ mode: 'transform' })` for the ESNext transpiles; what replaces the CommonJS transpile in `tests/guides.test.ts` that runs lifted helpers under `vm` (an ESM data-URL import, `vm.SourceTextModule`, or a rewrite of the lifting).
6. **Vue.** `vue-tsc` cannot run on 7 at all (it patches `typescript/lib/tsc`, which 7 removed) and no 7-capable checker exists today (research B). For a 7-only fleet: drop `vue-tsc` and `APP_BROWSER_TYPESCRIPT_RANGE`, check `app/browser` with `tsc` 7 over its `.ts` sources with a `*.vue` module shim, and record the loss of template type-checking in the guide until Volar reaches 7.1's API — or another shape. Decide and name what the generated workspace loses.
7. **Order.** `scaffold` first (vendored bytes and templates move), `probe` next (source), then every package: re-pin `typescript` to `^7.0.2`, drop the bridge, `repair`, regenerate `tests/distribution.test.ts`, rebuild, diff `dist/` against the published tarball, bump where it moves, publish in catalog layer order (`propagation-report.md`). State what a fleet visit does per package and which packages carry source work beyond the pair (`database`, `lsp`, `probe`).
8. **Retirement of the current state.** What the landed stage 1 and stage 2 (the bridge rows, the `typescriptCompilerFolder` override, `APP_BROWSER_TYPESCRIPT_RANGE`, the probe loader's fallback, the R1 to R3 roadmap rows) become under the break: deleted, transformed, or kept.
