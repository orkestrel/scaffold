## Evaluation criteria

Rank the options against these, each measurable from the option's own blocks plus the baseline table.

1. **Site delta per change kind.** For each cost-model row, the "after" file list against the baseline file list, member by member. An option that relocates a site without removing it scores zero on that row.
2. **Catch delta per change kind.** For each row, the named gate and the assertion that reds on a forgotten site, or `none`. A `toContain` presence guard scores `none` against a behaviour claim (`.claude/rules/documentation.md` § Parity).
3. **Check survival, including tautology.** Rule on SB, MB, LI, TE, NV, FL, EX, FI, and the executed fences individually: survives with an assertion that can still fail; becomes tautological because the option generates the side the check compares against; or moves, with its new home named. An option that generates `## Surface` and reports SB as surviving fails this criterion.
4. **Dependency delta.** New declarations named in block 1, measured against C1. A transitive package promoted to declared counts. An option reporting `none` while requiring `@microsoft/api-extractor-model` or a second api-extractor configuration is misreported, not cheap.
5. **Fleet cost per adopting package.** Files each package edits; whether the mechanism is vendored (`dist/host`, obliging a `scaffold` bump plus per-target re-pin, `repair`, and gates) or package-owned; whether it forces a runtime republish cascade through `@orkestrel/markdown` or `@orkestrel/template` (C8) or a devDependency re-pin alone (C7).
6. **Mirror safety.** Whether `guides/<name>.md` stays byte-identical to upstream `main` (C6), and whether a regeneration inside a target reds `scaffold audit`.
7. **Voice-law conformance of the produced bytes.** Whether the worked example shows a noun-phrase Surface row and a third-person TSDoc sentence produced from one source without either breaking its own rule (C9). Demonstrated on the example, not asserted in prose.
8. **Format stability.** Whether the produced bytes pass `npx oxfmt --config .oxfmtrc.json --check` on the first run, so regeneration and `format` do not fight (C2).
9. **Reversibility.** Whether abandoning the option means deleting a generator and keeping authored files, or whether authored prose was deleted and only the generator can restore it.
10. **Instrument honesty.** Whether every new check carries a negative control drawn from outside its membership rule, per `.claude/rules/quality.md` § Instruments, and whether the option states the check's coverage beside its result.
11. **Coverage of the uncovered.** Whether the option closes any of the three live drifts named earlier — the `birth` substance disagreement, the `Compiler` example disagreement, the `helpers.ts` voice violation — or leaves them exactly where they are. An option that reorganizes the covered surface and closes none of them has not addressed the defect class the repository actually carries.
