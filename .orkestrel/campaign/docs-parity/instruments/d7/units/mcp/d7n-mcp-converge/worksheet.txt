#### MCPStreamControllerInterface  (interface)
LIT   : `MCPStream` plus `stop()` and `[Symbol.asyncDispose]`
CLAUSE: a held-open answer whose cancellation ONE owner arbitrates, settling its consumer without waiting on the producer; what every stream leaving `dispatch` is. Ending it is the obligation of whoever is handed it, on EVERY exit, and there is no owner of last resort.
DOC   : Represents a held-open modern result whose cancellation ONE owner arbitrates — the arm ⏎ every stream leaving `MCPServer.dispatch` takes. ⏎  ⏎ @remarks ⏎ The generator protocol states what a stream yields and says nothing about who ends one, ⏎ and a native async generator answers that badly: `return()` and `throw()` QUEUE behind a ⏎ `next()` the producer has not answered yet, so a consumer walking away from a source ⏎ parked on an event that will never arrive waits forever for its own cancellation.
