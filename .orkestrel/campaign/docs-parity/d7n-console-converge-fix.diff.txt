 ### Logging
 
 Structured logging — the immutable `LogRecord` + the `entry` event are the transport seam; `Sink` is the one output primitive.

-| `LogRecord`              | interface | Represents one immutable, serializable log entry — a `level`, a `message`, and a `time`, plus an optional `name` and `data`.
