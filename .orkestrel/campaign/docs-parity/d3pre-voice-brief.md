## What the rules will read (fixed; D3 implements them, you converge to them)

- **`policy/no-imperative-summary`.** For every `/** … */` block that opens at a line start and is followed, on the next line, by an `export` declaration: the description paragraph is the block text from its opening to its first block tag (`@param`, `@returns`, `@remarks`, `@example`, and the rest), continuation markers `* ` removed, whitespace collapsed. Its first word must match `^[A-Z][a-z]*s$` and not sit in the stop set of words that end in `s` without being a third-person verb (`This`, `These`, `Its`, `Thus`, `Plus`, `Was`, `Alias`, `Bias`, `Canvas`, `Class`, `Status`, `Process`, `Access`, `Address`, `Assess`, `Compress`, `Express`, `Focus`, `Pass`, `Press`, `Discuss`, `Dismiss`, `Miss`, `Cross`, `Guess`, `Harness`, `Witness`, `Progress`, `Success`, `Series`, `Analysis`, `Basis`, `Business`, `Chaos`, `Lens`, `Bus`, `Gas`, `Yes`, `Always`, `Perhaps`, `Sometimes`, `Various`, `Numerous`, `Previous`, `Whereas`, `Unless`, `Across`). The first sentence (up to the first `. ` or the paragraph's end) must not contain the declared identifier as a whole word. `.claude/rules/typescript.md` gives the pattern: `Creates`, `Returns`, `Checks whether`. A tag line (`@param name - …`, `@returns …`) is outside this rule and keeps its noun-phrase form.
- **`policy/no-banned-term`** and the Markdown sweep. Prose is matched, case-insensitively and across inflections, against the rows of `.claude/rules/writing.md` § Substitutions whose ban is unconditional: `should`; `simply`; `easy` (`easier`, `easiest`, `easily`); `just`; `currently`; `utilize` (all inflections); `leverage` (all inflections); `via`; `in order to`; `e.g.`; `i.e.`; `etc.`; `performant`; `robust` (`robustly`, `robustness`); `allows you to`; `and/or`; `please`; `sanity check`; `dummy` (`dummies`); `blacklist`; `whitelist`; `slave`. The rows ruled by sense in review, outside the mechanical match: `now`, `new`, `latest`, `once`, `since`, `master`. Excluded from the match: fenced code blocks, inline code spans (a span can cross a line break), `{@link …}` targets, URLs. In TypeScript the rule reads every comment (line and block, doc or not); in Markdown the sweep reads every `.md` file in the workspace outside `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `tmp/`, `.orkestrel/`, and `.git/`, with the top-level guide mirrors (`guides/<other>.md`, every top-level guide that is neither `guides/README.md` nor `guides/scaffold.md`) excluded.

## What each rewrite must keep

- The sentence's claim. A rewrite changes the opener's form, never the fact: `The Oxlint context operations used by the policy rules.` becomes `Lists the Oxlint context operations the policy rules use.`; `Build a control …` becomes `Builds a control …`; `Whether the path is ambient.` becomes `Reports whether the path is ambient.`; `One diagnostic emitted by a policy rule.` becomes `Describes one diagnostic a policy rule emits.`; `Every centralized module named by the architecture kind table.` becomes `Lists every centralized module the architecture kind table names.`; `Inspect one discovered skill's …` becomes `Inspects one discovered skill's …`; `Ban framework mocking …` becomes `Bans framework mocking …`. Pick the verb the symbol's role earns (`Represents`, `Describes`, `Names`, `Lists`, `Holds`, `Reads`, `Reports`, `Checks whether`, `Creates`, `Builds`, `Writes`, `Inspects`, `Parses`, `Returns`), and keep the rest of the sentence's words wherever the grammar allows.
- The substitution table's replacement, by the row's sense: `via` → `through` or `by using`; `e.g.` → `for example`; `etc.` → bound the list or recast; `easy`/`easier`/`easiest`/`easily` → recast with the concrete property (`the most direct way`, `with the least construction`); `currently` → delete, or give the date where time matters; `just` → delete, or `only`/`alone` where the sentence needs the restriction; `should` → `must`, `can`, `might`, or the imperative. A hit inside a table cell or a heading is rewritten in place, keeping the table's column alignment (run `npm run format` afterwards; oxfmt re-aligns Markdown tables).
- The `.agents/**` and `.claude/rules/**` files are instruction files: keep every line a directive, and record no history of this change in them.
