chore: release mcp 0.0.31

- Publishes the browser face: `createPageServer` gives a page its own in-process MCP pair over a
  `MessagePort`, owning both transports, both binds, and the teardown; `createModelContext` bridges
  a tool registry to the document's WebMCP surface, publishing the registry's current tools,
  adopting the tools other origins expose, and following the registry live rather than registering
  a snapshot. The guide records that no shipping browser exposes the global, and that the proof
  drives an interface-faithful double.
- Publishes the server's `notifications/tools/list_changed` producer, driven by the tool registry
  itself: the subscription stream pulls its consumer on demand, coalesces a burst of registry
  changes into one frame, orders a failure after the frames already queued, and refuses a consumer
  filter that tries to omit the family the server produces from its own registry. Discovery now
  advertises `tools.listChanged`.
- Renames the conformance schema constant for its own contract.
- Re-pins `@orkestrel/tool` to `^0.0.15`, the registry release carrying the tool contract, and
  `@orkestrel/scaffold` to `^0.0.69`, whose vendored policy reader accepts an exported function
  overload.
- The distribution project gains the composition receipts: the packed artifact is driven in a real
  Chromium page beside the installed agent, tool, and parser artifacts, loaded over an import map
  answered from the consumer's own `node_modules`, with the page's requests recorded.

Campaign records live in the scaffold checkout under `.orkestrel/campaign/`.
