  `tests/setupPolicy.ts`) accepts an exported function overload signature (`TSDeclareFunction`)
  beside a function declaration, so a target whose source carries an overload passes its policy

  `buildModernResult`. The pin `reads each signature of an exported function overload` binds it,
  with controls that reach the declaration refusal (`export import`) and the statement refusals
  (`export default`, `export =`), and the export-case matrix carries a `function overload` row.
- The rule's collision consumer reports one name once per file per owner: an overloaded or merged
  export whose name a foreign guide claims raises one violation at its first declaration rather
  than one per signature line. The pin `reports an overloaded export's collision once` binds it.
- Refreshes the hosted guide mirrors from the repositories' pushed `main` where they moved since
  0.0.68 (tool, agent), and regenerates the `host.json` inventory; the surface record is unchanged.
