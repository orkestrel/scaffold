- The server owns the tools family: every `subscriptions/listen` stream whose honoured filter
  carries `toolsListChanged` yields one `notifications/tools/list_changed` per registry `add`,
  `remove`, or `clear`, from a handler parked on `tools.emitter` at the stream's start and
  released with `off` when the stream's signal aborts. No poll, no deadline read; a registry
  destroyed under a live stream publishes its final `clear` and then nothing.
- A consumer producer composes with the built-in one through one native `ReadableStream` queue
  (no installed `@orkestrel/*` primitive merges async iterables): the consumer's iterator advances
  only on the stream's demand, a producer failure terminates the stream after the notifications
  it already produced (the `-32603` terminal follows the queued frames), and registry changes
  that arrive while a `list_changed` frame is unread coalesce into it, so a parked reader bounds
  the queue by construction. The honoured filter is the consumer's `subscription.notifications`
  plus `toolsListChanged: true`; `MCPConsumerFilter` types what a consumer may declare, and a
  filter that claims the family, or one that is malformed, is refused at construction with
  `MCPError` `-32602`. A subscription opened on a destroyed registry is acknowledged and produces
  nothing, and a released subscription returns the consumer's iterator with the stream's abort
  reason, so a producer's pending writes reject with the reason rather than `undefined`.
