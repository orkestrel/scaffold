## Browser setup lessons

- The subject region is stored with the scenario at placement time, because the elements a journey photographs are gone when the written files are read back. `tmp/mikesaintsg-veneer/tests/setupBrowser.ts:60`
- A pixel reading is taken over the subject's declared region, because a reading of the whole frame answers for the surrounding page. `tmp/mikesaintsg-veneer/tests/setupBrowser.ts:89`
- The floor color separates a subject that paints one flat fill from a region that shows nothing, and omitting the floor reports the region's own variation. `tmp/mikesaintsg-veneer/tests/setupBrowser.ts:95`
- Two shots of one layout that differ by one declaration differ only where that declaration paints. `tmp/mikesaintsg-veneer/tests/setupBrowser.ts:151`
- The box is read while the pane is staged at the shot's geometry, and a page frame adds the document's scroll offsets because it covers the scrolled document. `tmp/mikesaintsg-veneer/tests/setupBrowser.ts:240`
