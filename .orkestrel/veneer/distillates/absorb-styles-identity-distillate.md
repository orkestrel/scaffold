## Motion facts

- **Factor token.** `--vn-factor-motion` starts at `1` and multiplies every duration token, and a factor of `0` or `2` is how a proof shows the duration scale. (`.orkestrel/veneer/e-id-motion-design-verdict.md:15`)
- **Duration tokens.** Feedback is `calc(150ms * var(--vn-factor-motion))` (`.orkestrel/veneer/units/e-id-motion-terrain-result.md:10`), panel is `calc(250ms * var(--vn-factor-motion))` (`.orkestrel/veneer/units/e-id-motion-terrain-result.md:11`), and slide is the added `calc(600ms * var(--vn-factor-motion))` (`.orkestrel/veneer/e-id-motion-design-verdict.md:24`).
- **Easing.** `--vn-ease-standard` is `ease`, `--vn-ease-out` is `ease-out`, and `--vn-ease-panel` is `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`, and the motion ruling gives `--vn-ease-out` and `--vn-ease-panel` their first readers. (`.orkestrel/veneer/units/e-id-motion-terrain-result.md:12`)
- **Reduced-motion gating.** The `transition` mixin writes the authored transition and a `transition: none` twin (`.orkestrel/veneer/units/e-id-motion-terrain-result.md:74`), and placeholder glow, placeholder wave, and both spinners take `animation: none`, with the grow spinner held at opacity `1` and the progress stripes left at `animation: none` (`.orkestrel/veneer/e-id-motion-design-verdict.md:30`).
- **Transition reader.** Style proofs use `sampleTransition` and `getAnimations()` to read the property, the resolved duration, and the easing (`.orkestrel/veneer/e-id-motion-design-verdict.md:37`); the engine reader `settleAnimations` awaits `finished` on those animations and does not parse a duration or listen for `transitionend` (`.orkestrel/veneer/units/e-id-motion-terrain-result.md:78`).
