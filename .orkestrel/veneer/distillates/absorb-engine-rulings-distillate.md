- Bootstrap event names include `show.bs.collapse`, `hidePrevented.bs.modal`, and `slid.bs.carousel`; `EventHandler.trigger` makes the pre-change event and the completed event cancelable; detail carries `relatedTarget`, `clickEvent`, `direction`, `from`, and `to`; a handler's `return false` cancels nothing. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:12`
- A dropdown's Bootstrap detail field is `clickEvent`, and the toggle that dispatches the event is `event.target`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:85`
- Modal listens for Escape `keydown` on its own element. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:17`
- Carousel's `slide(index)` queues until `slid` while a slide is in flight. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:16`
- A toast sits at z-index 1090 and a modal at 1055; backdrops are `.modal-backdrop` and `.offcanvas-backdrop` with `.fade` and `.show`; scrollbar compensation sets `overflow: hidden`, adds the scrollbar width to `padding-right` on `body`, `.fixed-top`, `.fixed-bottom`, `.is-fixed`, and `.sticky-top`, subtracts it from `margin-right` on `.sticky-top`, and sets `body.modal-open`; light dismiss is `mousedown` then `click` on the host; `backdrop: 'static'` fires `hidePrevented` and uses `.modal-static`; offcanvas contains focus only with a backdrop or when it does not scroll. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:18`
- The backdrop component receives a press whose target is the backdrop element itself. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:106`
- Placement strings include the centre modifiers; `display: 'static'` and a menu inside `.navbar` use `data-bs-popper="static"`; the cascade variable is `--bs-position`; `autoClose` distinguishes `inside` and `outside`; `boundary` and `popperConfig` are Popper options; `.dropdown-menu`, `.tooltip`, and `.popover` override position, margin, padding, border, and background. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:19`
- The sanitizer allowlist is Bootstrap's `DefaultAllowlist` (tags, per-tag attributes, and the globals `class`, `dir`, `id`, `lang`, `role`, plus `aria-*`); an unlisted element is removed with its content; `SAFE_URL_PATTERN` refuses `javascript:` and admits `data:`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:20` `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:33`
- Tooltip and Popover read no `data-bs-config` and refuse `sanitize`, `allowList`, and `sanitizeFn` from attributes; plugins otherwise merge defaults, `data-bs-config` JSON, and `data-bs-*` attributes. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:21`
- `isDisabled` is true for the component disabled token, the platform `:disabled` state, or a `disabled` attribute whose value is not `false`, and dropdown `show` reads that predicate on the toggle. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:73` `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:77`
- Bootstrap binds each handler with the element's `addEventListener` and writes state through `setAttribute` and `classList`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:243`
- Public members the engine mapped include `getInstance`, `getOrCreateInstance`, `VERSION`, `dispose`, `handleUpdate`, `setContent`, `toggleEnabled`, `prev`, `to`, `cycle`, and `nextWhenVisible`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:14` `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:22`
- The engine-utility surface named for disposal is `Manipulator.getDataAttributes`, `SelectorEngine.getSelector`, `getElementFromSelector`, `getMultipleElementsFromSelector`, `focusableChildren`, `Data.set` / `get` / `remove`, `enableDismissTrigger`, `getUID`, `getElement`, `isElement`, `isVisible`, `isDisabled`, `reflow`, `getNextActiveElement`, `TRANSITION_END`, `Config`, `isRTL`, `toType`, `noop`, `execute`, `findShadowRoot`, `getjQuery`, and `defineJQueryPlugin`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:25`
- The carousel `slide` class is declared by no Bootstrap cascade rule, and the script reads it to decide whether a move waits for a transition. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:114`
- Bootstrap's toast has no gesture dismissal. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:224`
- Bootstrap markup routes anchors and `data-bs-target` selectors that name several panels. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:193`
- The parity cascade renders a collapsed panel as `display: none`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:194`
- A horizontal collapse show writes `scrollWidth` at width 0, a horizontal hide writes the rect width, and `scrollWidth` / `scrollHeight` exclude the border, so a bordered `border-box` show stops short and jumps when the inline size clears. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:269` `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:275`
- Carousel adds `pointer-event` only when `'ontouchstart' in document.documentElement` or `navigator.maxTouchPoints > 0`, and its touch action is `pan-y`. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:363` `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:407`
- Button and ScrollSpy complete synchronously; dropdown dispatches `shown.bs.dropdown` and `hidden.bs.dropdown` without waiting on a transition and removes `show` at once on hide; tab waits on the pane transition; carousel waits on the active item; Alert, Collapse, Modal, Offcanvas, Toast, Tooltip, and Popover also wait on a transition before the completion event. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:384` `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/decisions.md:388`
- Tooltip's `data-bs-original-title` and Toast's hide token are Bootstrap surface the engine excluded. `.orkestrel/veneer/engine/j-engine-design-verdict.md:96`
