2. Run the Orchestrator's scoped gates (`tools/w2-gates-scoped.sh <unit> <round>`: typecheck, lint, format, the unit's test files, guides, policy).
3. Write the audit claims from the sources (`j-<unit>-audit-claims[-n].md`), then run the lanes on one claims file: `analyst` on Astra (`units/launch-codex.ps1 -Unit j-<unit>-audit[-n] -Brief <abs> -Sandbox read-only -Root C:/Users/mikes/WebstormProjects -Lane "<sentence>"`, watched by `tools/watch-codex.sh`, retained by `tools/retain-codex-last.cjs`) and `checker` on Sonnet; `reviewer` on Opus 5.5 at the landing round or where the shape changed. `tools/generate-w2-audit-briefs.py` and `tools/patch-landing-briefs.py` draft the lane briefs; read each start to finish before launch.
4. Reconcile into `j-<unit>-audit[-n]-verdict.md`. A fix that adopts the auditor's prescription closes on the instrument probe and the Orchestrator's replay (`tools/w2-replay.sh <unit> <round> [rows]`, run only when no lane reads the worktree).
5. Land: `tools/w2-land-1.sh <unit>` (commit with the landing message, merge `origin/main`, stop on conflicts; `units/j-w2-resolve-guide-2.py` and `tools/resolve-surface-table.py` resolve guide conflicts), or `tools/w2-merge-main.sh <unit>` where the unit's commits are already on its branch; then `tools/w2-land-2c.sh <unit> [hold]` (the merge commit, a reinstall where the merge moved the lockfile, the gate chain, and, without `hold`, the fast-forward), and `tools/w2-land-3.sh <unit>` for a fast-forward an audit held. A unit whose change is Markdown no test but policy, format, and guides reads lands through `tools/land-doc.sh`. The chain runs `format:check`, `lint:check`, `check`, `test:guides`, `test:policy`, `test:src:browser`, the three builds, `test:conformance`, `test:setup`, and `test:setup:browser`; a unit that touches `app/**` or `tests/setup*` adds `test:app`, `test:journey`, and `build:app`. A non-fast-forward push means the styles session landed first: merge again, re-run, push again. A conflict in `guides/veneer.md` or `ROADMAP.md` resolves in favour of the owning session.
6. Push Veneer `main`, retain the landing log, update § Intersession state, and commit the records (`tools/commit-records-2.sh <messagefile>`: stages `.orkestrel/veneer/engine` by pathspec, commits, merges `origin/main`, pushes). At the next landing boundary, prune the landed unit's records per `orkestrel-debrief` § retention.

Tests widen with the work: a writer runs the one file (or one case) that pins the open obligation, then the files its edit reaches, and the whole browser suite once before its report; the Orchestrator runs the landing chain once on the merge result.

## Exit criterion

The design verdict's § Exit criterion (`j-engine-design-verdict.md`, its enumerated capabilities, items 1 to 9), checked against the kickoff brief's § Acceptance criteria (`../j-engine-session-brief.md`, items 1 to 6, whose item 3 asks lifecycle, cancellation, focus, motion, and cleanup proved per component). Every unit is judged against Veneer's `ROADMAP.md` § Tenets as well; the J-TENETS audit of 2026-09-24 (`units/j-tenets-*-report.md`) is the latest reading, and E26 adds to the exit criterion, on the user's ruling, that every `plugin` row's proof compares against an independent recording of Bootstrap's bundle (J-ORACLE).
