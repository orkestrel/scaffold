## Landing procedure

Per D43, E4, and ROADMAP § Protocol › § The engine session: a unit writes in a temporary worktree `veneer-<unit>` on `unit/<unit>` cut from the committed `main` tip (`units/unit-worktree.sh new`), commits there on a pass (`unit-worktree.sh commit`), and lands by cherry-pick onto `main` (`unit-worktree.sh land`, which first fast-forwards `main` to `origin/main` and removes the worktree and branch after). Before every landing, `git fetch origin main` in Veneer and scaffold, read `git log --oneline <marker>..origin/main` in both, read the baseline's `plan.md` § Intersession state and `units/decisions-round-2.md`, and move the marker; where `origin/main` moved, `main` takes it by fast-forward before the cherry-pick (a merge commit only when `main` already carries an unpushed engine landing). After the cherry-pick, one independent `verifier` runs the gate chain on `main` (`npm run format:check`, `lint:check`, `check`, `build`, `test`, `test:service`), the Orchestrator compares every red row against `units/host-chromium-153-reading.md` (E5), and a green reading pushes `main`; a non-fast-forward push means the baseline landed first, so fast-forward or merge again, re-run, push again. A conflict in `guides/veneer.md` or `ROADMAP.md` resolves in favour of the owning session. A records commit in scaffold stages `.orkestrel/veneer/engine` by pathspec, commits, fetches and merges `origin/main`, and pushes `main` (`units/commit-records.sh`).
