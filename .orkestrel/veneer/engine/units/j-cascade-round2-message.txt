Bound each fade engine's completed event to one frame at a zero and a large motion factor

The motion-factor cases showed the event follows the fade, but bounded no arrival, so an engine waiting a fixed second passed them. Each case now requires that, at a zero factor, the event and the call's resolution arrive before one frame, and that, at a large factor, the event arrives after the fade's `finished` and before the next frame.

The mutation instrument counts a kill only when the failure is an assertion. It kills a fixed wait and a late wait in all five cases, and it refuses a thrown `Error`.
