Sanitize tooltip and popover markup on every Chromium, with or without setHTML

`NativeSanitizer` becomes `ConfigSanitizer` (E21). Each write reads whether the target carries the platform's `setHTML` method and uses it when it does. Where it does not, as on Chromium 141, the markup is parsed in an inert document inside a shallow copy of the target, walked against the same `SanitizerConfig` and a declared safety floor, and moved in with `replaceChildren`, never serialized again. `SANITIZER_BASELINE` is the floor: `base`, `embed`, `frame`, `iframe`, `object`, `script`, and SVG `script` and `use` go with their content; every `on…` attribute goes; a `javascript:` URL the platform's URL parser reads in `href`, `action`, `formaction`, or `xlink:href` goes; an animation's `attributeName` naming one of those goes. The walk reads every name, attribute, child, and removal through the platform prototypes, and the document through `Document.prototype`, so a form's named controls, or a connected element named `implementation`, shadow nothing it reads. A configuration that is not plain data is refused before it is copied. A target the floor names is refused on both routes, and a target from another window is sanitized as in this one.

Every tooltip and popover now works on a Chromium without `setHTML`: before this, each show threw there, text-only tips included. Every case runs through the host's own route and a forced walk against one literal; the walk-only removals are named in `SanitizerBaseline` and pinned with their own literals; two platform-conformance cases compare the floor and the refusals with the platform where it carries them. The `isSanitizeTarget` case no longer asserts a host fact. The design round ran on Opus and Astra; the landing audit and its re-audits (Opus, Astra, and the checker) found and closed a context-parse difference, DOM clobbering, a construction hole, and a document-level read; the Orchestrator re-ran the mutation instrument, whose control row survives, after each round.
