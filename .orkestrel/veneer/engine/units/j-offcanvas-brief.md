**The obligations (each an edit and a proof; red first where the behaviour is new).**

- **OFF1 Construction and options.** `Offcanvas` validates the host, resolves the groups and the options (`backdrop` through `parseBackdrop` as the modal reads it, `keyboard` and `scroll` through `parseBoolean`) in the contract's order, claims the host, binds the hooks and its Escape listener, and writes nothing. `shown` reads the `shown` token on every access.
- **OFF2 Show and hide.** Mirror `Modal.ts` door by door with the differences the distillate item 2 names: `show` refuses while shown, in flight, or destroyed; dispatches the cancelable `show.vn.offcanvas`; locks the scroll when `scroll` is false; shows the `Backdrop` (always animated, painted on the host's parent with the `offcanvas-backdrop` tokens the group names) when `backdrop` is not `false`; writes `aria-modal="true"` and `role="dialog"`; adds `showing`, reflows, adds `shown`, removes `showing` when the transition settles (`settleAnimations`, honouring reduced motion through the cascade); constructs the `Isolation` (the focus trap) when `backdrop` is true or `scroll` is false; dispatches `shown.vn.offcanvas`. `hide` reverses it: `hide.vn.offcanvas`, the isolation released, `hiding` added, `shown` removed, the settle, `hiding` removed, the attributes restored, the backdrop hidden, the lock released, `hidden.vn.offcanvas`; `toggle` calls the other. A static backdrop click (`backdrop: 'static'`) or Escape with `keyboard` false dispatches `prevent.vn.offcanvas` with the modal's detail shape and hides nothing; Escape with `keyboard` true hides; a backdrop click hides. Bootstrap's blur-on-hide, its load auto-show, and its hide of a shown modal are excluded on the record (the distillate names them unnamed by the contract); state each in the guide.
