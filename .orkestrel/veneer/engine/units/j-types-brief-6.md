- **E19 (claim 4).** Rename `ScrollSpySelectorMap.link` to `parent`, its TSDoc "Selects the links that precede a nested list and turn active with a link inside it, Bootstrap's `SELECTOR_LINK_ITEMS`; the tracked links are the host's `[href]` links, which `link` reports. On `TooltipClassMap.auto` and `PopoverClassMap.auto`, the TSDoc states that the token is written on every tip whatever `placement.position` names ("Marks every tip whatever `placement.position` names, so the cascade places the arrow by the side the tip resolved to. - **E20 (F1).** Reduce each composite selector default to the part no key of its entity names, and state the composition in its TSDoc: `DropdownSelectorMap.trigger` becomes `[data-bs-toggle="dropdown"]` ("the engine matches `:is({trigger}):not(.{disabled}):not(:disabled)`"); `DropdownSelectorMap.entry` becomes `.dropdown-item` ("the engine matches `{menu} :is({entry}):not(.{disabled}):not(:disabled)`"); `TabSelectorMap.link` becomes `.nav-link, .list-group-item, [role="tab"]` ("the engine matches `:is({link}):not({toggle})`, and `:is({link}, {trigger})` where Bootstrap reads `SELECTOR_INNER_ELEM`"); `ScrollLockSelectorMap.fixed` becomes `.fixed-top, .fixed-bottom, .is-fixed` ("the padding set is `:is({fixed}, {sticky})`").
