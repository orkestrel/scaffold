## Findings outside the claims (the subjective lane), each ruled

- **F1 — composite selector defaults embed another key's token, so an override does not follow it.** `DropdownSelectorMap.trigger` embeds `:not(.disabled)`, `DropdownSelectorMap.entry` embeds the menu selector and the disabled token, `TabSelectorMap.link` embeds the toggle selector, `ScrollLockSelectorMap.fixed` embeds the sticky selector. Real, under the derive-state law and the amendment's intent. Ruling: each member carries only its foreign part (`trigger: '[data-bs-toggle="dropdown"]'`, `entry: '.dropdown-item'`, Tab `link: '.nav-link, .list-group-item, [role="tab"]'`, ScrollLock `fixed: '.fixed-top, .fixed-bottom, .is-fixed'`), the engine composes the rest from the resolved keys with `:is()` and `:not()`, each TSDoc states the composition, and R19's membership rules gain the sentence "a composite selector's own-token part is derived, so a member carries only the part no key of the entity names" (folded into `j-engine-design-verdict.md` § Amendments and `j-engine-shape-verdict.md` in the same commit).
- **F2 — unchanged sentences state fixed tokens the same file makes replaceable** ("carries the `show` class" on the `shown` getters, "adds the `show` class" on Backdrop, "the `pointer-event` class" on Swipe, "carries the `active` class" on Tab, and the guide cells). Real: under an override each sentence is false, and the fixed wording is a marker of the earlier shape (E6). Ruling: every such sentence takes the token language ("carries its shown token"), with the guide cells following; a sentence identifying a component by its default markup ("a `.collapse` panel") stays.
- **F3 — `selector` beside `selectors` on `TooltipOptions`.** Real: a singular and plural pair with unrelated meanings on one object. Ruling: the delegation option and its attribute key become `descendants` ("Delegates the tooltip to descendants of the host matching this selector, mirroring Bootstrap's `selector` option"; the attribute default stays `data-bs-selector`); R11's path list follows.
- **F4 — the overlay's backdrop tokens have no replacement path.** Real under E9. Ruling: the overlay passes its `backdrop`, `shown`, and `fade` tokens to Backdrop's `host`, `shown`, and `fade`, stated on the `ModalClassMap` and `OffcanvasClassMap` leaves and in `BackdropClassMap`'s description; `OffcanvasClassMap` gains `fade` (Bootstrap's offcanvas backdrop is always animated, `offcanvas.js:180-183`).
