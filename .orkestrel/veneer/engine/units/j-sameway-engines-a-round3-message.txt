Record and return a taken-over change's writes through four shared helper leaves

Collapse, Tab, and Carousel each carried an almost identical record, read, and rewind set, and Toast its own record. They now share four exported, tested leaves in helpers.ts:
- readHostValue reads a target as HostSnapshot's save reads it, with absence as undefined.
- writeHostValue writes a target.
- recordHostChange records a target once, at its first changing write, and nothing for a write that changes nothing.
- rewindHostChanges restores each recorded target in reverse order, skipping one already at its value, and stops when the call no longer owns the change.

HostChange, the entry shape, is declared once in types.ts. Each engine keeps only its #rewind boundary, which carries the call's identity into the leaf.
