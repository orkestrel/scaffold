### Outside my tenets

1. **Refer to the objective lane.** The `engine \| event` row claims Bootstrap cancels an event when a handler returns `false`. R2 of the design verdict, read against `dom/event-handler.js`, states that Bootstrap's wrapper does not honour `return false`. So the Obligation cell may misstate Bootstrap itself, not only Veneer. J-ROWS would carry the fix.
2. **For the user.** All three tables in the guide's ledger — the CSS table under § Additions, § Departures, and § Compatibility — measure the cascade. No ledger records engine additions or engine departures. They live only in prose under § Engine, so a consumer who reads § Compatibility alone sees `shipped` against behaviour Veneer does not provide. Whether an engine departure table belongs in the ledger is a design choice outside J-ROWS's cells.
