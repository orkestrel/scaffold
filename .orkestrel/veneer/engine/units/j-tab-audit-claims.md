The first planned write for an element's attribute wins (`#absent`), and a write whose value the element already carries is dropped. A control outside any list plans nothing. Pinned by: "writes the initial roles and states on the list, its controls, their wrappers, and their panes, and destruction restores them", "writes each list-group item its own wrapper, taking no presentation role", "constructs a control with no list, writing nothing, and activates it and its pane with no sibling to hide", "claims the host and resolves its pane by the target attribute before its href", "refuses an invalid host and a second owner, then permits reconstruction after destruction", "refuses a group value that is not a class token, an attribute name, or a selector before claiming the host", "destroys the tab when its signal aborts, and at construction when it arrived aborted", each with its instrument rows.
