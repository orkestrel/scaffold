Land J-TOOLTIP: the Tooltip engine, the native sanitizer, the placement arrow, and their proofs

`Tooltip` (`src/browser/Tooltip.ts`) shows a tip built from a template beside its trigger in the top
layer through `Placement` promoted with the `hint` popover value, on hover, focus, click, or call,
with one native timer for the `delay` group, the active-trigger record Bootstrap keeps, the
`descendants` containers, the `title` moved into the tip's content and `aria-label` written as
Bootstrap's `_fixTitle` does, `aria-describedby` naming the tip while shown, the modal's hide
event honoured, and a door read after every write, dispatch, content call, element move, and await.
`NativeSanitizer` (`src/browser/sanitizers/`) writes markup and the template through the platform's
`setHTML` over `SANITIZER_ALLOWLIST`, Bootstrap's allowlist by element with every WAI-ARIA name
listed; a supplied `SanitizerInterface` replaces it, and no writer skips sanitizing. `Placement`
gains an `arrow` input it points at the reference's centre from the edge facing it. The tip helpers
`buildTip`, `fillSlot`, and `writeContent`, the parsers `parseDelay`, `parseTrigger`,
`parsePosition`, and `parseFallbacks`, the guards `isTooltipEvent` and `isSanitizeTarget`, and the
frozen `TOOLTIP_*` tables land with them. An element a slot receives returns home on destruction,
on a refill, and on a rebuild that supplies another.

Departures the guide records: the `.vn.` names; completed events not cancelable; `animation` as
`animated` and `customClass` as `tip.class`; the trigger words as the `trigger` group; `sanitize`,
`sanitizeFn`, `allowList`, `boundary`, and `popperConfig` without a counterpart; the platform's
`aria-*` list without Bootstrap's pattern; `hide` and a leave working while disabled; `toggle`
immediate; the hint's platform dismissal bridged after the close with one re-promotion (E17 as
amended).
