It claims the host in the static `Registry` (`OFFCANVAS_HOST_OWNED`), binds the hooks through `bindEventMap` with `isOffcanvasEvent`, listens for `keydown` on the host, `mousedown` on the owner document, and `resize` on the window under the controller's signal, destroys at construction when `signal` arrived aborted and on its abort otherwise, and writes no token or attribute. `shown` reads the `shown` token on each access. Cases: "writes nothing at construction, binds its hooks, Escape, and press listeners, and reads the shown token on every access", "resolves the backdrop, the dismissal, and scrolling from the attributes, the constructor winning path by path", "refuses an attribute value that fails coercion…", "refuses a group value…", "refuses an invalid host and a second owner…", "destroys the panel when its signal aborts, and at construction when it arrived aborted".
