## Rulings per claim

1. **Construction, the menu, and the options — CONFIRMED** by every lane.
2. **Show, hide, and their doors — FAIL.** The objective lane: a `beforetoggle` listener that cancels the native opening leaves the menu not `:popover-open`, yet `Placement` completes and the dropdown's door (lifetime, identity, the menu token) passes, so the show writes the tokens, dispatches `shown`, and resolves `true` over a closed popover. Ruling: the placement reports a cancelled promotion (it restores what it wrote and throws `PLACEMENT_PROMOTION_REFUSED`), and `#reveal` catches that one code, drops the placement, and resolves `false` before any token write. Also from that lane: `#reveal` destroys an old placement with no door before constructing the new one. Every other door held under both lanes. Item A.
3. **Position and the placement mechanism — FAIL on D5; D4 held.** The objective lane: a `beforetoggle` listener that destroys the dropdown leaves `Placement` writing inline positioning, the anchor name, the observer, and the listener after `destroy()` returned, which R1 ("no mutation after `destroy()`") forbids; the subjective lane weighed D5 as acceptable on cost, and the law wins. Ruling: `PlacementOptions` gains `signal`; the placement reads it after the popover attribute write and after `showPopover()` (the reaction-capable steps) and, when aborted, restores what it wrote and stops; the dropdown passes its controller's signal. D4 (`position: fixed`) held in both lanes with the tall-page control. The Orchestrator's claim mis-attributed the `data-popper-placement` prefix selectors to `_dropdown.scss` (they belong to `_popover.scss` and `_tooltip.scss`; the dropdown cascade selects on `data-bs-popper`); that error is the claim's, not the unit's, and writing the side alone stands on the popover and tooltip cascades' prefix selectors. Items A and B.
4. **Light dismissal (D1) — CONFIRMED.** Both lanes rule the engine-owned dismissal the right shape: `types.ts` gains no `hide(click?)` and no readable `dismiss`. The subjective lane's Ref3 (a menu shown by markup with no engine show closes on an outside click, where Bootstrap also requires the toggle's `show`) is ruled no change: the menu's token is the dropdown's state, as the `shown` property states.
5. **The delegate's routes and E12 — FAIL.** The objective lane: the key route's entries pass `checkVisibility()` with no options, so a `visibility: hidden` entry takes focus where Bootstrap's `isVisible` reads the computed visibility; the Escape route focuses the toggle after `engine.hide()` without re-reading the delegate's lifetime, so a `hide` listener that destroys the delegate is followed by a focus write; the nested-roots case binds the click mark alone (a prevented hide would make the key mark observable); the listener-count row changes the event name and keeps the count. Both lanes: the disabled-toggle order is not a defect (Bootstrap's toggle selector excludes disabled toggles, so it leaves their default action alone too), and the Orchestrator's claim premise was wrong. The subjective lane's F1: the key route navigates with the delegate's `entry` selector, so a consumer-constructed dropdown's own `entry` has no effect. Ruling: `entry` is a delegate-only key, stated on `DropdownOptions.selectors` and `DropdownSelectorMap` as `ButtonOptions.selectors` states its own; no `step` member is added (Minimal public API), and the keyboard stays the delegate's (R5), with a departure stating that a dropdown constructed directly answers no key (the subjective lane's Ref4). Item C.
6. **The guard, the tables, the parsers, and the helper — CONFIRMED on the mechanism; three bindings unproved.** The positive offset case, the invalid static-value case, and the frozen `Placement` tables have no red row. Item E.
7. **The guide and the returned patch — FAIL.** Both lanes: the placement paragraph's alignment sentence is false for a centered menu (`center.*` returns before `--bs-position` is read); the `Dropdown` summary and § Surface row say "to the toggle" where the reference can be the parent or a given element; "promotes every nonstatic menu" and the successful-show sentence are false for a cancelled promotion; the keyboard sentence promises a move the hidden-entry vector prevents; the D5 sentence describes a behaviour item B replaces; the class `@remarks` state the Tab rule wrongly (F5); "flips and shifts" claims a shift nothing writes (Ref2); one sentence lost its commas. The § Delegation hunk stays out (W5). Item D.
