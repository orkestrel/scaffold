1. **Construction, the menu, and the options.** `Dropdown` (`src/browser/Dropdown.ts`, one class plus imports) conforms to `DropdownInterface` (`host`, `menu`, `shown`, `show`, `hide`, `toggle`, `update`, `destroy`, the static `find`): the constructor refuses a non-`HTMLElement` host with `DROPDOWN_HOST_INVALID` carrying `readTag(host)`; resolves the `classes` group with its nested `center` group (both through `resolveVocabulary` with `DROPDOWN_OPTION_INVALID`, the result frozen), the `attributes` group, and the `selectors` group; locates the menu (`#locate`: the first `HTMLElement` the `menu` selector matches among the toggle's following siblings, then its preceding siblings, then inside its parent) and throws `DROPDOWN_MENU_MISSING` when none; resolves `dismiss` through `resolveOptions` with `parseDismiss` (the constructor's `dismiss` group replacing the attribute's reading whole, a key it omits taking `DROPDOWN_DEFAULTS.dismiss`), resolves the `placement` group as its own layer through `resolveOptions` over `DROPDOWN_DEFAULTS.placement` with `parseOffset` and `parseStatic` (key by key), and resolves the reference as `options.reference ?? #refer(host)` (`data-bs-reference` `toggle` or `parent`, `parent` requiring an `HTMLElement` parent, any other value throwing `DROPDOWN_OPTION_INVALID`), all before the claim; claims; binds the hooks through `bindEventMap` with `isDropdownEvent`; binds `click` and `keyup` listeners on the toggle's document for the lifetime (D1); honours `signal`. It writes nothing at construction. `destroy` returns when destroyed, aborts, releases the claim before restoring, destroys the placement, and restores the `shown` tokens of the toggle and the menu and the toggle's `aria-expanded`. Pinned by: "finds the menu after the toggle, then before it, then inside its parent", "refuses a group value that fails validation, a declared attribute that fails coercion, and a toggle without a menu, before claiming the host", "refuses an invalid host and a second owner, then permits reconstruction after destruction", "binds its hooks, the document listeners, and the signal, and destroys the dropdown when its signal aborts, and at construction when it arrived aborted", "closes the open menu without events on destruction and restores the toggle and the menu, keeping consumer edits", "offsets the menu from the offset attribute and anchors it to the reference the attribute or the constructor names", each with its instrument rows.

2. **Show, hide, and their doors.** `#refused(shown)` reads the lifetime, a change in flight (`#change !== undefined`), the menu already carrying the state (`this.shown === shown`), and the toggle matching `.disabled, :disabled`. `show` refuses (`false`, no event) on `#refused(true)`; dispatches `show.vn.dropdown` (cancelable, detail `{ click: undefined }`) and refuses on prevention; re-reads `#refused(true)`; takes a `#change` identity; then (`#reveal`) saves the `shown` tokens and `aria-expanded`, destroys any placement, constructs a `Placement` over the reference and the menu (`popper` and `side` attribute names, the position from `#position`, the offset, static when `placement.static` or the toggle sits inside a `navbar` match), destroys it and resolves `false` when the dropdown was destroyed inside that construction, reads the door (`#holds(change, false)`: live, identity, the menu lacking `shown`), then writes behind `#apply`: `host.focus()` and `aria-expanded="true"` with the door at `shown = false`, the menu's `shown` token, the toggle's `shown` token, and `placement.update()` with the door at `shown = true`; clears `#change`; dispatches `shown.vn.dropdown`; returns `!aborted`. `hide` (`#conceal(undefined)`) mirrors it: `#refused(false)`, `hide.vn.dropdown` with `{ click }`, the re-read, the identity, the save, the placement destroyed, the door at `shown = true`, then the menu's token removed, the toggle's token removed, and `aria-expanded="false"` each behind the door at `shown = false`, `#change` cleared, `hidden.vn.dropdown` with `{ click }`, `!aborted`. `toggle` calls `hide` when `shown` else `show`; `update` forwards to the placement. The write order matches Bootstrap's `show` (`_createPopper`, focus, `aria-expanded`, menu `show`, toggle `show`) and `_completeHide` (popper destroyed, menu `show` removed, toggle `show` removed, `aria-expanded`, `popper` data attribute removed). No door admits a state the call then writes over; while a change is in flight every other call resolves `false` at once (so a `focus` listener calling `hide` inside the show leaves the show to complete); a listener that shows again inside `show.vn.dropdown` runs the one show and the outer call resolves `false` (E15 does not apply: two directions). Pinned by: "opens the menu in the top layer below the start of its toggle and closes it again, keeping the toggle in step", "refuses to show or hide a disabled toggle, to show an open menu, and to hide a closed one, writing and dispatching nothing", "leaves the toggle and the menu untouched when a listener prevents show or hide, and resolves false", "dispatches bubbling events on the toggle, the pre-change ones cancelable, each carrying an undefined click, and binds only engine-shaped events to hooks", "stops writing when a reaction to its own write destroys it, resolving false and restoring the toggle and the menu", "writes nothing when a listener to its show event destroys it, resolving false", "promotes once and dispatches one shown event when a listener to its show event shows it again, the inner call resolving true and the outer false", "resolves false for a call a listener makes while a change is in flight, and completes the change once", "stops and demotes the menu when a listener to the toggle focus or the menu promotion destroys it, resolving false", "stops a show whose menu token write a reaction answers by removing that token, dispatching no shown event", "stops a hide whose menu token removal a reaction answers by adding that token back, dispatching no hidden event", "measures and rewrites the side of the open menu at an update", each with its rows.

3. **Position and the placement mechanism.** `#position` reads the toggle's parent tokens as Bootstrap's `_getPlacement` does: `end` → `right-start`, `start` → `left-start`, `center.up` → `top`, `center.down` → `bottom`, then the menu's computed `--bs-position` equal to `end` → `top-end` or `bottom-end` under `up` or otherwise, else `top-start` or `bottom-start`. `Placement` (`src/browser/Placement.ts`, one class plus imports) conforms to `PlacementInterface` (`reference`, `element`, `side`, `update`, `destroy`): it refuses a non-`HTMLElement` reference or element with `PLACEMENT_INPUT_INVALID`, resolves the `attributes` group with `PLACEMENT_OPTION_INVALID`, and, when static, saves and writes `static` to the `popper` attribute and does nothing else; otherwise it refuses a disconnected element, reads the computed `POPOVER_PROPERTIES` before promotion, saves and sets `popover` to `input.popover ?? 'manual'`, calls `showPopover()`, writes back each property promotion changed as an inline declaration, then writes inline (each saved first) `position: fixed`, every inset `auto`, the offset as margins (the distance on the side facing away from the reference, the skid along it), `position-anchor`, `position-area` from `PLACEMENT_AREAS[position]`, `position-try-fallbacks` (`flip-block` for a vertical side, `flip-inline` otherwise, `none` for an empty list, else the listed areas), `position-try-order: normal`, appends a generated `--vn-placement-N` name to the reference's computed `anchor-name` list, observes the reference with a `ResizeObserver`, listens for `scrollend` on the document in the capture phase, and runs `update()`. `side` is `undefined` when static, destroyed, or the element fails `checkVisibility()`, else the side whose gap between the two rectangles is widest; `update` saves and writes the `side` attribute (`data-popper-placement`) to that side; `destroy` aborts, disconnects, hides an open popover, and restores every attribute and declaration it wrote, leaving the reference's prior anchor names. The shipped `_dropdown.scss` selects `[data-popper-placement^="top"]` and its siblings by prefix, so writing the side alone satisfies the cascade. Pinned by the twelve behavioural `Placement.test.ts` cases and "takes the side and edge from the wrapper direction tokens and the menu alignment property", "leaves the menu in flow with the static popper attribute inside a navbar or under a static display, and removes it at hide", "reads the same border, padding, background, color, and overflow on the promoted menu as on the menu the cascade shows in flow", each with its rows. D4 and D5 are the design questions here: whether `position: fixed` is required for the flip against the viewport (the writer's probe: an `absolute` menu on a scrollable page did not flip, a `fixed` one did), and whether a placement without a `signal` and without per-write doors is acceptable when the dropdown reads its own door after the construction returns.

4. **Light dismissal (D1).** `#lightDismiss` runs on every document `click` and `keyup` while the menu is shown: a `keyup` whose key is not `Tab` is ignored; a secondary-button click is ignored; an event whose composed path includes the toggle is ignored; an event whose path includes the menu is ruled by `dismiss.inside`, any other by `dismiss.outside`; inside the menu a `Tab` release and a click on an element whose tag name matches `/input|select|option|textarea|form/i` are ignored; otherwise `#conceal(click)` runs with the click (or `undefined` for a `Tab` release) as the `hide` and `hidden` detail. This matches Bootstrap's `clearMenus` for the one open menu the instance owns (the right-button and non-Tab `keyup` returns, the toggle exclusion, the `autoClose` matrix, the Tab-inside and form-control exceptions, the `clickEvent` detail). The design question for the lanes: D1 puts these two listeners on the document for the lifetime of every dropdown instance, shown or not, where Bootstrap binds two on the document once for its data API; the writer's reason is that `DropdownInterface` exposes no `dismiss` member and `hide()` takes no click, so a delegate could neither read a constructed engine's rules nor pass the click. Rule whether the engine-owned dismissal is the right shape, or whether `types.ts` must gain `hide(click?: MouseEvent)` and a readable `dismiss` so the delegate owns it. Pinned by: "closes on a click outside and on a click inside the menu, carrying the click, and on a trusted Tab that leaves the menu", "keeps the menu open for a click on its toggle, a secondary button, a form control inside it, and a key other than Tab", "closes only on the clicks its dismissal allows, read from the auto-close attribute or the constructor", each with its rows.
