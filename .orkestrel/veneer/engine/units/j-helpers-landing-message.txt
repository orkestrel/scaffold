Land J-HELPERS: the engine's DOM traversal and reading helpers, extracted and routed

`src/browser/helpers.ts` gains `readClosest` (the closest HTML match, the element itself included,
bounded by an optional root), `readSiblings` (one side's matching siblings, nearest first),
`readOutermost` (the matches no other match inside the root holds), `readScrollbarWidth` (Bootstrap's
scrollbar measure, 0 for a viewless document), and an optional root on `readTarget` that bounds the
first match alone. Every duplicate site routes through them: the delegate's routes and conflict
preflight (`#closest` and `#readModal` are gone), the tab's list and wrapper reads (`#list` is gone),
the dropdown's menu lookup, the delegate's toggle lookup, the scrollspy's parent walk, the collapse's
sibling panels, the carousel's items, and the modal's and scroll lock's scrollbar measurements.

The disabled predicate is `matchesDisabled` (E16 as amended at the J-HELPERS design round: a new
`is*` name is a type guard under `.claude/rules/names.md`, and `matches*` is the predicate prefix).
The scrollspy's link reading and the dropdown's own `show`, `hide`, and `toggle` route through it, so
a directly constructed anchor toggle carrying a `disabled` attribute whose value is not `false` is
refused by those calls, as Bootstrap's `Dropdown.show` and `hide` read it, while light dismissal
closes its open menu as Bootstrap's `clearMenus` does; the delegate's dropdown-entry keyboard filter
keeps its declared selector. `readOutermost` judges nesting on the matches themselves, so a
`Document` or `DocumentFragment` root reads as an element root does.

The guide's § Surface gains the five rows and folds its duplicate `computeNeighbor` row; § Delegation,
`#### Dropdown`, and `#### ScrollSpy` name the shared reading. `types.ts` takes the
`DropdownSelectorMap.trigger` TSDoc patch the unit returned.
