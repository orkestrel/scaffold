Land the shared engine mechanisms on Button: registry, snapshot, events, motion, targets, and the option merge

`Registry` records one owner per host per class; `Snapshot` records the state an engine first
touches and restores it on destroy; `emitEvent` dispatches the cancelable bubbling event and
returns whether it was prevented; `bindEventMap` binds an entity's hooks by its wire table;
`settleAnimations` waits on `getAnimations()` and `finished`, re-reading until none runs;
`reflow` forces the layout read; `readTargets` resolves a trigger's target selector with
`CSS.escape`; `resolveOptions` layers defaults, declared attributes, and the constructor object
through the contract package's parsers. `Button` migrates onto them, and `Delegate` drives any
engine and releases removed hosts at observer delivery. J-BINDER round 1, audited by Astra,
Opus 5.5, and the checker; round 2 adapts it to the amended contracts. J-ENGINE campaign (D43).
