**The obligations (each an edit and a proof; red first where the behaviour is new).**

- **TIP1 Construction and options.** `Tooltip` validates the host, resolves the `classes`, `attributes`, and `selectors` groups and the options in the contract's order (the constructor over the attributes over `TOOLTIP_DEFAULTS`: `animated`, `delay.show`, `delay.hide`, `trigger.hover`, `trigger.focus`, `trigger.click`, `title`, `html`, `tip.template`, `tip.class`, `container`, `placement.position`, `placement.offset`, `placement.fallbacks`, `descendants`, `sanitizer`), claims the host, binds the hooks and the interaction listeners its `trigger` group selects, moves the host's `title` attribute out of the platform's reach for the tooltip's life (Bootstrap's `_fixTitle`: the value becomes the default title content and the host gains `aria-label` where it has no label and no text; rule and record the exact behaviour under Unknown 1), and writes nothing else. `enabled` starts `true`; `enable`, `disable` flip it; a disabled tooltip's `show`, `hide`, and `toggle` resolve `false` and its interactions do nothing.
