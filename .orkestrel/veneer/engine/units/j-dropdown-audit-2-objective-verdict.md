**Findings fitting no claim**

**F1 — Arrow navigation focuses an entry after delegate destruction.** Construct a dropdown directly, then construct a delegate covering its toggle. Attach a `shown.vn.dropdown` listener that destroys the delegate and focuses another connected button. Dispatch ArrowDown from the toggle. `engine.show()` dispatches `shown` synchronously before returning (`src/browser/Dropdown.ts:224`). Delegate destruction leaves the manually constructed dropdown alive because only acquired engines are owned (`src/browser/Delegate.ts:172`). The arrow route then continues without reading its lifetime and focuses the first visible entry, replacing the listener’s focus (`src/browser/Delegate.ts:278`, `src/browser/Delegate.ts:289`). This violates the destruction boundary already enforced in the Escape branch at line 275.

Smallest fix: read the delegate’s lifetime immediately after `engine.show()` and stop before entry navigation if destroyed. Preserve navigation within an already-open menu: `show()` returning `false` alone is not a reason to suppress arrow navigation. Do not destroy a manually owned engine to conceal the defect.
