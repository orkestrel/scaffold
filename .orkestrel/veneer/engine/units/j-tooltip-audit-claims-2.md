3. **Show and hide (TIP3).** `show` resolves `false` when the tooltip is destroyed, a change is in flight, the tooltip is disabled, the host is disconnected or carries an inline `display: none`, or no slot has content (`#refused`, `#blocked`, `#filled`); it takes the change identity before dispatching the cancelable `show.vn.tooltip` (`null` detail) and refuses on prevention; it reads its door (`#holds`: live, the same change, the same tip) after the dispatch, after discarding an earlier tip, after building, after `aria-describedby`, after appending to the container (also that the tip's parent is the container), after `inserted.vn.tooltip`, after the placement, after the `shown` token, and after the settle; the container is the option, else the closest `[aria-modal="true"]`, else the body; the placement is `new Placement({ reference: host, element: tip, popover: 'hint', arrow? }, { attributes: { side }, position, offset, fallbacks, signal })`, and a `PLACEMENT_PROMOTION_REFUSED` throw discards the tip and resolves `false` while any other error propagates; it reflows, adds `shown`, awaits `settleAnimations` when `animated`, clears the change, dispatches `shown.vn.tooltip`, and resolves `!aborted`. `hide` resolves `false` when destroyed or not shown, takes the change identity, dispatches the cancelable `hide.vn.tooltip`, clears the active-trigger record, removes `shown`, awaits the settle when `animated`, discards the placement and the tip (and the id from `aria-describedby`), dispatches `hidden.vn.tooltip`, and, when a pointer or focus entered during the hide and no timer is armed, shows again; a hide started while a show's tip carries `shown` takes the show over through the change identity.
