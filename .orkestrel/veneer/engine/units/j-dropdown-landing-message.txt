Land J-DROPDOWN: the Dropdown and Placement engines and the delegate's dropdown click and key routes

The Dropdown engine over the landed binder: construction resolves the toggle's menu, the dismissal
and placement options, and the vocabulary before the claim, and writes nothing; showing dispatches
the show event, restores a placement a taken-over show left, constructs a Placement over the menu
(anchoring it to the toggle or its reference through the native popover and anchor positioning,
refusing the show when a listener cancels the promotion), focuses the toggle, writes aria-expanded
and the shown tokens behind doors that read the lifetime, the call identity, and the toggle's
disabled state, and dispatches shown; hiding dispatches hide, destroys the placement (its
restoration completing before any destroy returns, whatever call is in flight), removes the tokens,
and dispatches hidden; dismissal listens for outside clicks, Escape, and Tab per Bootstrap's
autoClose rule; destruction releases the claim, aborts (the placement holds the signal), and
restores the toggle. The Placement engine: static or promoted, a preferred position with fallbacks
and an offset, updated on scroll end and resize, restoring what it wrote on destruction or refusal.
The delegate routes toggle clicks and the ArrowUp, ArrowDown, and Escape keys from a toggle or a
menu, moving focus among the enabled visible entries while the delegate is still live. The parsers
(parseOffset, parseStatic, parseDismiss), the guards, the frozen tables with DropdownVocabulary and
DropdownDefaults, the barrel exports, and the guide's Dropdown subsection with its plugin row
shipped. Three rounds: the round-1 audit by the analyst on Astra, the reviewer on Opus, and the
checker (units/j-dropdown-audit-verdict.md); round 2 by the analyst on Astra and the checker
(units/j-dropdown-audit-2-verdict.md); round 3 by the analyst on Astra and the checker
(units/j-dropdown-audit-3-verdict.md); the landing round merges main and folds the shared delegate
members. J-ENGINE campaign (D43).
