Declare the engine contracts: event maps, hooks, options, interfaces, and the shared mechanisms

`src/browser/types.ts` declares every contract the J-ENGINE design verdict fixes (R2 to R14):
the `EventHooks` and `EventWire` helper types, the registry, snapshot, isolation, backdrop,
scroll-lock, placement, swipe, and sanitizing contracts with their options, and for each of the
eleven components its detail, event map, hooks, options, and interface, with every property
readonly, every state-changing verb returning `Promise<boolean>`, and an `@example` on every
method. The guide's § Surface carries one row per export and § Methods one table per behavioural
interface. The seed's declarations are unchanged except the `ColorModeInterface.toggle` summary,
which states the post-destroy return, and `ButtonHooks`, which becomes the `EventHooks` alias
of its event map. Audited in two rounds (Astra objective, Opus 5.5 subjective, Sonnet checker):
round 1 split the placement group per entity, made `fill` return the promise, restated four
refusal lists from Bootstrap's source, and renamed ScrollSpy's active link to `link`; round 2
carries two refusal sentences to J-TOOLTIP and J-DROPDOWN as their `types.ts` patches. `SetHTMLOptions` and `SanitizeTargetInterface` mirror the
`setHTML` method the installed DOM library omits. J-TYPES, W0 of the J-ENGINE campaign (D43).
