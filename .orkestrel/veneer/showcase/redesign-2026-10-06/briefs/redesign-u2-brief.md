## Behavior (`app/browser/Showcase.ts`)

The `details` element's `open` attribute is the single source of the drawer's state. Add the drawer logic to the mount (every listener on the mount's `AbortController` signal; `destroy` leaves no listener and no pending completion):

- **Sync.** A `toggle` listener on the `details` reads `open` and drives the panel. Opening: the panel gains `showing`; when its `transform` transition ends (`transitionend` on the panel itself with `propertyName === 'transform'`), `showing` is swapped for `show`. Folding: the panel gains `hiding` (keeping `show`); at the transition's end `show` and `hiding` are removed. Where the panel's computed `transition-duration` is 0 s (reduced motion, or from `lg`), the swap completes in the same task with no transition classes left. A route that fires while a transition runs cancels the pending completion (one pending completion at a time); a `transitioncancel` event completes the pending step. The summary swaps `text-body-emphasis` for `active` while open, as the pressed choices do.
- **Fold routes**, each setting `open` to `false` so the `toggle` listener folds: (1) a click on a link inside the panel, with the default hash navigation left to run; (2) an Escape `keydown` on the document while open, which also focuses the summary; (3) a `focusout` from the panel whose `relatedTarget` is an element outside both the panel and the `details` (a null `relatedTarget` keeps it open); (4) a document click outside both the panel and the `details`; (5) `#offset` reading the panel's computed `position` as not `fixed` while open (the viewport reached `lg`), which clears `show`, `showing`, and `hiding` at once.
- `#offset` keeps writing `scrollPaddingTop` on the root and `top` and `max-height` on the one `.sticky-lg-top` element, which is the panel.
- Opening leaves focus on the summary; the panel follows the header in DOM order, so the next Tab reaches the first Contents link.
