## 2. Pseudo-elements

`collectPreflightPseudos(css: string): readonly string[]` strips comments, takes each non-`@` rule selector, and returns the sorted unique `::` names (`tests/setupStyles.ts:439-448`). The preflight text is `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css` (`tests/integration.test.ts:310`), and the pinned result equals `preflightRecord.pseudos` (`tests/integration.test.ts:327`, `tests/fixtures/tailwindcss/preflight.json:74-95`): `::-webkit-calendar-picker-indicator`, `::-webkit-date-and-time-value`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-day-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-fields-wrapper`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-hour-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-meridiem-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-millisecond-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-minute-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-month-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-second-field`, `::-webkit-datetime-edit-year-field`, `::-webkit-inner-spin-button`, `::-webkit-outer-spin-button`, `::-webkit-search-decoration`, `::after`, `::backdrop`, `::before`, `::file-selector-button`, `::placeholder`. Those names are the selectors at `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:6-11`, `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:248`, `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:279`, `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:287-298`, `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:315-362`, `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:379`, and `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:387-389`.

`collectPseudos` hardcodes `::before` and `::after` when `content` is neither `none` nor `normal`, plus `::marker` on `display: list-item`, `::placeholder` on an input or textarea with a placeholder, and `::file-selector-button` on `input[type=file]` (`tests/setupBrowser.ts:657-673`). It never calls `collectPreflightPseudos`.

The preflight names it does not collect are the fifteen WebKit names and `::backdrop`. `::-webkit-search-decoration` is the rule at `node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:315-317`; the page's search fields are the navbar tracking field (`app/browser/sections/navbar.html:46-51`) and the input-group shipment field (`app/browser/sections/input-group.html:26-31`). The datetime and calendar pseudos (`node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:324-362`) and the spin buttons (`node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:387-389`) have no `date`, `time`, `datetime-local`, `month`, `week`, or `number` input in `app/browser`. `::backdrop` is on the universal preflight selector (`node_modules/tailwindcss/preflight.css:6-11`); the archive dialog is a `div.modal` (`app/browser/sections/modal.html:37-43`), and `app/browser` has no `dialog` element. `::marker` is the opposite gap: `collectPseudos` emits it, and preflight does not.

Derive the candidate list from `collectPreflightPseudos` of that preflight text, keep a name when the mounted element generates it (`::-webkit-search-decoration` on `input[type=search]`, the datetime and calendar names on a date or time input, the spin buttons on `input[type=number]`, `::backdrop` on a top-layer dialog, and the four names already gated), and keep `::marker` for `list-item` because preflight never yields it. The proof at `tests/setupBrowser.test.ts:237-246` should assert that candidate list equals `collectPreflightPseudos(preflight)`, add `<input type="search" placeholder="Find">` to the fixture, and expect `['::-webkit-search-decoration', '::placeholder']` for that field while the existing six expectations stay.
