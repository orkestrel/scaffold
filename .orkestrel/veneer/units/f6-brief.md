### Obligation 1 — colour-mode islands (audit claim 5)

removes the attribute to express `light`. Construction captures the host's original attribute
value (or its absence) and `destroy` restores exactly that; the stored boolean flag that recorded
"applied" goes, because the captured original derives it. Proofs in `tests/src/browser/ColorMode.test.ts`:
light applied inside a dark island renders the light body colour on the island (read through the
installed cascade readers); a root that carried `dark` before construction reads `dark` again after
`apply('light')` and `destroy`; a root that carried nothing reads no attribute after `destroy`. Run
the island case red before the engine change and record the command and its failing count. Correct
the guide's § Surface sentences on `apply` and `destroy`.
