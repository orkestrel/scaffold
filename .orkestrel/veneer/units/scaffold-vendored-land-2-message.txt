Permit a literal color in the token file alone, and restage the host inventory

The styles rule says the one file a literal color may appear in is
`_tokens.scss`, where the token itself is declared; the earlier wording
named a "value map" no rule defines. The rule is vendored, so `host.json`
is restaged with its digest. The inventory also carries the surface
policy's styles side-effect entry from `e8a34296`, committed after the
0.0.75 release; against the published host set the tip differs by those
two digests and by no path.

This lands as a vendored-only release: every target re-pins and repairs
after it publishes. The directory-index accounting drafted against the
policy is withdrawn unlanded; a package documents itself in one guide,
and the stray-guide rule stands as published.
