Scale the resting-key journey case's timeout with its key table

The journey case that reads every resting cascade key on its lifted frame shoots one frame per key,
so its run time grows with the CASCADE_KEYS table. Under capture it took 105 s of the journey's fixed
120 s budget at load 3.3, and under a loaded verification chain it timed out and, still running, failed
every later case in its run. The case takes the table's length times the CASCADE_KEY_TIMEOUT
constant, 3000 ms per key, declared beside the table.

B-CROSS support unit JOURNEY-BUDGET: `builder` on Sonnet, verified by `checker`.
