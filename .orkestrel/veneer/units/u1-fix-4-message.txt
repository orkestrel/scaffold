Scan the shipped cascade for direction-sensitive declarations through postcss

Replaces the text-pattern RTL guard with a scanner over a postcss parse
of the built cascade: it walks every declaration, lowercases the
property, splits values at the top level so a parenthesized group stays
whole, ignores the important flag, and returns the first physical
inline-axis longhand, side-keyword text-align or background-position,
asymmetric edge shorthand, or asymmetric border-radius as the
declaration itself. The property sets and the splitter are exported and
proved on every edge form the audit named, and the cascade case refuses
an empty or unbuilt file before scanning.

Audit round 3 (scaffold/.orkestrel/veneer/u1-fix-audit-verdict.md)
named each item; the report's prose names members instead of counts.
