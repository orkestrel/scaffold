Add the journey verbs, the pseudo-element readers, and the media stage

`@orkestrel/test/browser` gains `hoverAccessible`, `holdAccessible`,
`releasePointer`, `stageMedia`, `releaseMedia`, and `sendProtocol`,
with `MediaOptions`, `POINTER_HOLD`, and `MEDIA_STAGE`; `readStyle` and
`readPixels` take a pseudo-element argument guarded by the `::` prefix
and `CSS.supports`.

The hold maps the control's centre through the tester iframe's painted
scale, sends the press through the provider's DevTools session, records
its marker only after the press send resolves, reads `:active` back, and
releases before refusing a miss. The release keeps the marker until its
send resolves, so a rejected send is retried.

The media stage records the readings it observes before its first stage
on a root marker and re-sends the unnamed axes with each stage, so a
provider override survives; the release restores exactly the recorded
readings as explicit emulation and waits per axis for them, because a
reset that waits for stability has no knowable target. A refusal on
either path restores what it carried in and reaches the caller with the
cleanup failure attached as its cause.

Every voice, bound, and pattern is in the guide with its transcribed
case; the controls carry red and green logs of one command each; the
gates are green on managed Chromium and on Edge.
