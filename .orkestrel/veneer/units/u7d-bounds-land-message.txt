Restate the paint wrappers after Test-paint and close the U7d bounds

The installed `@orkestrel/test` reader parses the modern colour spaces
after Test-paint, so two cases in `tests/setupBrowser.test.ts` that
asserted its old limits went false and the whole chain went red. The
canvas and the reader were measured on 2026-09-20 and agree on every
colour tried, out-of-gamut ones included (both clip to the sRGB cube);
the one reading `readPaintedColor` still adds is the engine's own
rasterization, one byte per channel. The doc blocks say that now, the
two cases assert agreement with the installed reader, and a third case
pins the out-of-gamut clip. The wrappers stay for the styles proofs
that consume them; a later unit removes them.

From the U7d audit: `collectShippedComponents` lists a component only
when every selector row and every variable row it carries is shipped,
with a split-category case; the working-directory case asserts the
cascade's digest against `BOOTSTRAP_CSS_DIGEST` instead of re-deriving
the reader's path and names its dependence on the `setup` project's
`forks` pool; the pinned-release case reads the manifest and the cascade
through the manifest-rooted constants; an incomplete deferral row is
refused by its position and first non-empty cell; every binding with a
named obligation must match a compatibility row, and the `Dispatches
click` binding no row reached is deleted, its two cases rewritten to
prove the refusals the table carries.

Two planted controls reddened and were restored byte for byte; the
brief's gates exit 0 on managed Chromium and Edge.
