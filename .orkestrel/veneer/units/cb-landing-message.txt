Scope the elements layer's calibrated button surface to the bare button

The universal button rule writes the release's reboot at the release's values, and the calibrated
surface with its hover, active, focus-visible, disabled, and reduced-motion branches reaches only the
bare button: a button with no class attribute and no data-bs-target attribute. A component's button
form therefore paints as its anchor form does: a disabled nav link, a dropdown item, a list-group
action, a carousel indicator, and the close control take their component rules and the reboot alone.
A classed or utility-classed button in consumer markup takes the reboot alone as well; the guide
says to add the btn class for the Button treatment. The showcase's mode control is hooked by a
data-control attribute, and the proofs read each component's button form against its anchor form.
