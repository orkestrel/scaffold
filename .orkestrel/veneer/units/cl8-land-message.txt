Ship the grid rows, columns, and offsets (CL8)

The published cascade carries the grid: the row with its gutter aliases and
negative margins, its direct children taking their share of the gutter, the
row-column counts, the flexible and automatic and numbered columns, and the
offsets, each unconditioned and at every breakpoint the ramp carries.

Every selector the pinned inventory records under the three keys ships at the
condition it records, and every emitted declaration carries the recorded
value once the logical-property ruling is applied. Compiling the styles entry
unminified and comparing against the record leaves one difference: the
breakpoint-scoped zero offset emits a percentage zero where the record has a
unitless zero, which resolves identically.

Two of the keys are prefix buckets rather than clean keys, so this unit ships
the grid selectors and records the rest. The row key also carries every
row-gap utility, which CL8b owns because they share a generation rule with
the gutter step utilities. The col key also carries three form-label classes,
which belong to Forms and which no form key records, so the col key is their
only record. Each withheld name has its own deferral row naming the unit that
closes it, and the presence scan refuses a deferred name found in the
cascade.

One loop over the breakpoint ramp emits both the unconditioned and the
breakpoint-scoped variants, taking an empty infix at the zero boundary, and
the offset's zero member exists only in its breakpoint form because that is
what the record carries. The column widths are percentages of the grid's own
division rather than a retunable scale, so this unit adds no token.

The generated vocabulary is bound to its sources. The setup proof asserts the
grid tables' breakpoint infixes against the compiled ramp, the column steps
and the row-column counts against the inventory, and the whole generated
selector set against the inventory minus the withheld families, so a ramp
boundary or an inventory step added later reddens instead of shipping a class
with no reading. That binding is the finding CL7's audit carried here.

The declaration blocks the grid would have shared with the container moved to
the mixins file, because the standing sweep forbids a block shared by two
partials and the honest fix reaches both copies. The container's emitted
cascade is unchanged, proved by comparing the built declarations before and
after with negative controls on the comparison itself, and its proof is
untouched and passing.

Every gate exits 0 on managed Chromium, and the styles, browser-setup, and
app-browser projects exit 0 on Edge. The audit is recorded in scaffold's
`.orkestrel/veneer/cl8-audit-verdict.md` file.
