Ship the toast key with its section, proofs, and guide rows

The toast key ships whole in its own partial: the toast box and its custom properties, the shown,
showing, and hidden states, the container and its gap, the header with its close-control combinator,
and the body, every length and colour read from the tokens and the body aliases. The toast and the
container each declare the stacking slot over the toast rung, and the container applies the level, so a
retune moves every container and the toasts inside it. The Toast region renders the release's toasts
inside viewport frames placed with shipped utilities, with the flex-grow class standing in for the
margin utility that does not ship and no coloured toast, and records the declined showing frame. The
style proof reads every recorded selector, slot, state, geometry, and paint against the official
inventory, and retunes each slot's token alone to prove the slot reads that token even where another
token declares the same length. The close combinator moves from the deferred table to the shipped one,
the case tables live in the setup file, frozen and bound by derivation, and the guide carries the toast
section, ledger, compatibility, and plugin rows.
