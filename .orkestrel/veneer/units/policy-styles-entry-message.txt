Accept the styles side-effect entry in the surface policy

The workspace rule names src/styles/index.ts as a side-effect entry that
imports ./index.scss, while readPolicySurface treated every src index
module as a barrel and required a relative .js star export on each line,
so a target that followed the rule failed its policy gate. The reader now
validates that entry on its own: exactly one bare import of ./index.scss,
contributing no surface target, with a named violation for any other
shape. The proof covers the accepted entry beside a normal barrel and the
rejected star-export control, red before the change and green after.

Found while adopting @orkestrel/veneer (scaffold/.orkestrel/veneer,
unit U1-author run 3, 2026-09-20).
