Why it matters: `Delegate` accepts an arbitrary `root` (`src/browser/types.ts:60-63`), so overlapping roots and a consumer-held `Button` are ordinary usage, and Bootstrap's own handler reuses an instance rather than refusing (`node_modules/bootstrap/js/src/button.js:61`).
