Land the Content/layout tokens and the breakpoint mixins (CL2)

The space ramp gains `--vn-space-12` and `--vn-space-24` under its index
law, the type scale gains `--vn-display-1` through `--vn-display-6` at
Bootstrap's display steps outside the density factor, and the theme
closure gains `--vn-state-stripe` beside the hover and active percentages.
Each name lands in the registry and the cascade together, and the
bidirectional equality proof reddened on a planted unmapped name.

The breakpoint widths now have one Sass source: the `breakpoints` function
in the mixins partial, which the tokens partial walks to emit
`--vn-breakpoint-*` and the `--bs-breakpoint-*` aliases, and which the
`breakpoint-up` and `breakpoint-down` mixins read for their range-syntax
conditions. A name the ramp does not carry fails the build. The zero
boundary wraps nothing upward and emits nothing downward. The proof reads
each emitted condition out of the loaded fixture and compares it with the
resolved token, drives the viewport below, at, and above every boundary,
and reddened on a one-pixel move of the source. A media-condition reader
joins the browser setup module and a width parser the styles setup module.

Every gate exits 0 on managed Chromium and Edge. The audit is recorded in
scaffold's `.orkestrel/veneer/cl2-audit-verdict.md` file.
