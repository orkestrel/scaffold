## Findings

16. **Required change. `guides/veneer.md:172`, `:194`, `:202`, `:212`, `:226` — five tables sit bare under their headings.** `.claude/rules/writing.md` § Structure: "In a reply or a guide, introduce every list, table, and code fence with a complete sentence naming what follows." `#### Text and surface`, `#### Links`, `#### Type`, `#### Space, border, radius, and elevation`, and `#### Motion, focus, validation, breakpoints, and stacking` each drop straight into a four- or five-column table. Failure scenario: a consumer landing on `#### Links` meets `Token | Light | Dark | Source | Alias` with no sentence saying the rows are mode-dependent or that `Source` reads through the legend seventy lines earlier, while the two sections that do carry an opener taught them to expect one. Right: one sentence under each of those headings naming what the table lists and what its columns hold, in the form `#### Factors` (`:105-109`) and `#### Palette and gray ramp` (`:125`) already use.

17. **Required ruling. `src/styles/_mixins.scss:28` — `role-each` is a mixin with one caller in the shipped source.** Its only include under `src/styles/**` is `src/styles/_mixins.scss:60`, inside `theme-tokens`. `.claude/rules/styles.md:46` — "A one-partial pattern stays inline; do not create a mixin for one caller" — is the rule brief 7 item 4 applied to delete `theme-assets`, whose only include was in `_theme.scss`'s dark scope. The remaining include at `tests/src/styles/fixtures/mixins.scss:33` is a test of the mixin and cannot justify the mixin's existence. Failure scenario: the next unit adding a role tier opens `_mixins.scss`, finds one one-caller mixin deleted under `styles.md:46` and another kept beside it, and cannot tell which reading the project holds. Right: inline the `@each` into `theme-tokens` and drop the fixture's include, or record in the file the reason `role-each` is retained where `theme-assets` was not. `reduced-motion` is not the same case — `.claude/rules/styles.md:48-49,58` names it as the project's own mechanism — and it stays.
