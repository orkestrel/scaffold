Record each added declaration's value, report every unattributed rule, and derive the shipped keys from the inventory (LEDGER-ADDITIONS)

The cascade ledger's Addition rows carry the declared value beside the component, name, condition, and category, and
collectAdditions reports every emitted rule that no shipped key claims instead of skipping it. The shipped component
list derives from the pinned inventory rather than a hand-written list. collectAttributionClasses reads the classes a
selector writes at its own level or inside an :is() or a :where() argument, decoding escaped names, so a rule written
through those pseudo-classes attributes to its component.
