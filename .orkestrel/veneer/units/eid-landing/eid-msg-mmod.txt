Move the modal dialog and both backdrops on Elements' panel motion (E-ID-MOTION-MODAL)

A fading dialog rests at scale(0.96) and grows to none over --vn-motion-panel
on --vn-ease-panel as the show class joins, and the static bounce's
scale(1.02) runs on the same transition. The modal host fades its opacity over
--vn-motion-panel on --vn-ease-out from the .modal rule, which wins over the
shared .fade rule by load order at one specificity, and the overlay-backdrop
mixin writes the same fade on each backdrop's .fade compound, from 0 to 0.5
with no blur, so the offcanvas backdrop takes it too. Each proof reads the
running transition through sampleTransition, asserts a sample is present
before reading it, and reads a doubled duration at a doubled motion factor and
none at zero or under reduced motion. The guide records the dialog's entrance
as a departure and the host and backdrop fades as additions, and the Offcanvas
engine paragraph names the backdrop's timing by its token.
