Emit anchored visibility on the open promoted overlays (E-ID-ANCHOR)

The anchor-visibility mixin writes position-visibility: anchors-visible on
the open popover state of one class, and the dropdown, tooltip, and
popover partials each include it once in the components layer (D47,
D47a). The open overlay computes that value on Chromium 141 and 153 alike;
neither paints an open overlay whose trigger a scroll container clips
entirely, except Chromium 141 when the engine showed a menu after a
trusted pointer press on its toggle, which the engine leaves unanchored
there (D47b). The guide states each and records the additions. Four
rounds (anchor-audit-verdict.md to anchor-audit-4-verdict.md, PASS on
round 4).
