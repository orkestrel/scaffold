Keep dl, blockquote, and figure defaults on the tag, and let Bootstrap's classes carry Bootstrap's layout

E-ID-LAYOUT (E-IDENTITY, Addenda 2 and 3). A dl keeps its two-column grid, a blockquote its bar, inset, and italics,
and a figure its caption spacing, whatever class the tag carries; no selector reads a tag's context or whether it
carries a class. Bootstrap's classes write the layout their patterns need: a .row column's gutter overrides the term
padding so dl.row lays out as Bootstrap's horizontal description list, .blockquote clears the bar, and
.figure-caption clears the caption margin. figure returns to block flow, so the attributed quotation ends at its
footer and the following content sits where Bootstrap places it. The proofs read each pattern's geometry, each red
under its named mutation, and the probe reads every pattern against Bootstrap 5.3.8 at 390 and 1280 pixels.
