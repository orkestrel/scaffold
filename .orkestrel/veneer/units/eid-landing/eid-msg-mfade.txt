Fade on Elements' ease-out, and read a running transition through one shared reader (E-ID-MOTION-FADE)

and in the Fade classes prose. sampleTransition in tests/setupBrowser.ts returns the CSSTransition the browser runs for
a named property, with its duration, its easing, and the computed value at its start and midpoint, and refuses a
transition that carries no effect; its proof in tests/setupBrowser.test.ts reads a real transition, a control whose
transition is removed, and the no-effect refusal. The fade proof drives the show class off and on and reads the
running transition's duration, easing, and midpoint frame, the doubled duration at a doubled factor, and no transition
