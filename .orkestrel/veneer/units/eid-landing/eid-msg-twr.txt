Hold the preflight recipe to a compiled fixture and read the component classes under it (TAILWIND-RECIPE)

tests/fixtures/tailwind/consumer-preflight.css is the executed copy of the
guide's preflight recipe, and a case holds the guide fence to it line for line.
The consumer proof compiles each recipe with its @source line and reads a
utility the markup names, and a recipe without that line fails the reading.
The component proof reads every Veneer component class that shares a longhand
with Tailwind's preflight, and each resolves the same under the recipe as under
the cascade alone. collectMovedLonghands in tests/setupServer.ts reports every
moved longhand of each reading, and the layer order, control variables, and
source lines the proofs read live in tests/setupService.ts. Guide § Tailwind
states that each recipe's import leaves automatic source detection on, and that
only the workspace compile roots it at an empty directory.
