+// Returns a size scaled down below the `xl` boundary by the release's responsive font size rule,
+// with its 1.25rem floor and its factor of 10. A zero-width viewport resolves the floor plus a tenth
+// of the size's excess over it, the other nine tenths of the excess return in proportion to the
+// viewport width, and the whole size is back at the boundary. The `max()` guard leaves a size at or
+// under the floor fixed at every viewport, so a size token retuned across the floor switches between
+// the fluid and the fixed behavior at runtime. The boundary is interpolated, because Sass would
+// otherwise divide the viewport length by it and refuse the result.
+@function fluid($size) {
+	@return calc($size - max($size * 0.9 - 1.125rem, 0px) * (1 - 100vw / #{breakpoint(xl)}));

+@mixin font-size($size) {

+	@include breakpoint-up(xl) {
