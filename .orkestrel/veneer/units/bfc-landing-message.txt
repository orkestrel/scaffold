Land the form check classes as a Bootstrap 5.3.8 baseline

Unit B-FORMS-CHECK on Opus over two rounds, audited by analyst on Astra,
reviewer on Opus, and checker on Sonnet, the fix round by analyst on Astra.

- src/styles/components/_form-check.scss emits every recorded form-check
  selector: the box, the radio, the mixed state, the switch, the reverse and
  inline layouts, and the disabled treatment, with the glyphs read from the
  $icons map through one @each for the checkbox and radio pair, the dark
  switch knob from the $dark map on a component-level descendant rule (D28:
  a light island nested in a dark one keeps the dark knob, as the release's
  own rule does), the filled states on the blue palette entry so the baked
  white glyphs stay legible in either mode, the focus ring on the focus
  tokens and the focus border on the palette tint, and the knob transition
  through the motion mixin with its reduced-motion twin.
- tests/src/styles/components/form-check.test.ts reads every treatment in
  the browser, the disabled label through an attribute-only host that
  separates the [disabled] half from the :disabled half, the glyphs against
  the release's own URIs through FORM_CHECK_ICON_CASES keyed by mode, and
  the ring after keyboard traversal in each mode.
- The Form check showcase section, ten resting scenarios, the focus page
  frame, and the mixed element frame (indeterminate joins CaptureState),
  the compatibility rows, the listed entry, and the form-check ledger table.
- The theme scope's switch-knob entry stays in $assets until B-FORMS-ASSETS
  removes it with the select caret (D26).
