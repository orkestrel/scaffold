Share the form controls' type and border declarations through two mixins

B-FORMS-MIXIN (`builder` on Sonnet, D40) adds `control-type` and `control-border` to
`src/styles/_mixins.scss` and routes `.form-select` and `.input-group-text` through them, so the
declarations the release derives from its `$input-*` variables have one home and the duplication
floor (D32) reads the shared runs as one mechanism rather than a repeated block; the cascade
compiles byte-identical to the block form (the writer's `cmp` record, with a swapped-order control
that differs). The CONTROL landing routes `.form-control` through the same mixins.

Records: `.orkestrel/veneer/units/b-forms-mixin-*` and `bfm-*` in the scaffold repository.
