Bound every capture frame to its opening, its section, and its lifted specimen

The FrameManager class takes each main child that holds neither the
subject nor the frame out of the layout for the shot, and restores
exactly the hidden attributes it set in a finally block. It stages the
pane at the viewport, reads the bounded content height, stages the pane
again at the taller of the two on every path, and reads the region only
when a second reading agrees. It refuses a frame whose area in device
pixels exceeds FRAME_AREA (1280 x 41954, the largest frame a run has
read back), naming the scenario and its size. The scenarios getter
derives from the recorded placements.

The page strip's hover and focus frames become element frames of their
padded stage, and every pointer-held placement sits on a lifted
specimen behind a structural guard that fails an in-place host. The
un-lift mutation reddens the primary-hover case on that guard; without
the guard it passes while its frame paints the rest fill.

At light-1280 the showcase frame is 1280 x 800 and the tallest page
frame 1280 x 1648, under the decode ceiling every 1280 page frame
exceeded before; the bottom offcanvas region is read at the viewport
the shot uses.
