Tooltip: no data API; `placement: 'top'` and `trigger: 'hover focus'` defaults; `show` and `hide` methods; cancelable `show.bs.tooltip` and `hide.bs.tooltip` events; an `id` attribute a trigger's `aria-describedby` attribute names; placement writes the `bs-tooltip-auto` class and `data-popper-placement` attribute; sets the `show` class, and the `fade` class when the tip is animated; `Sanitizer` and `TemplateFactory` utilities. Owner: J-ENGINE.
Popover: extends Tooltip, inheriting its methods and ARIA under `.bs.popover` events; `content: ''`, `placement: 'right'`, and `trigger: 'click'` defaults; the release removes the `.popover-header` or `.popover-body` element when its title or content is falsy; placement writes the `bs-popover-auto` class and `data-popper-placement` attribute; sets the `show` class, and the `fade` class when the popover is animated. Owner: J-ENGINE.
