Route every reduced-motion reading and selector split through one shared source

CLOSE-MOTION (`builder` on Sonnet, one round) exports the `REDUCED_MOTION` query from the styles
setup module, retires each proof's own copy of the literal and every inline one, derives the case
tables' expected media conditions from it, pins its value, and routes every selector-list split in
the floating and pagination proofs through the shared `splitTopLevelList` helper (rulings R3 and
R9).

Audited by `analyst` on Astra and `checker` on Sonnet. Records: `.orkestrel/veneer/units/cm-*`
and `close-motion-*` in the scaffold repository.
