Judge a three-value radius by the corners a flip swaps

A three-value border-radius sets the top-left, the top-right and
bottom-left pair, and the bottom-right; a right-to-left flip swaps
top-left with top-right and bottom-right with bottom-left, so the side
is direction-sensitive when the first value differs from the second or
the third differs from the second. The previous successor compared the
first with the third, following a fixture the brief had labelled the
wrong way; the fixture is now flagged and two more pin each corner pair.
