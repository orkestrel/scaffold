Ship the Button engine and the delegated data API behind ./browser (U7b)

`Button`, one class over a `<button>` or anchor host, derives `pressed`
from the host's `active` class, and `toggle()` writes the class, then
`aria-pressed`, then dispatches the non-cancelable bubbling
`toggle.vn.button` event and returns the state, as the official engine
orders its mutations on both hosts. Hooks bind through `on` with
`bindEventMap`; `destroy()` restores the class membership and the
attribute exactly as found, releases the hooks and the ownership, and is
idempotent. A second `Button` over a host already owned is refused, as is
a host that is not an element of the current realm; both refusals throw
`AppError` with a machine-readable code and the host's tag in context.

`Delegate`, one class a consumer's own entry constructs, installs one
delegated native `click` listener on its root, resolves the host through
`closest('[data-bs-toggle="button"]')`, prevents the default, refuses a
disabled host, drives one engine per host from a private map, leaves a
host owned elsewhere to its owner without an error escaping the listener,
prunes on each click every engine whose host has left its root, and
releases the listener and every engine on `destroy()`. Importing
`./browser` registers no listener; the package's surface stays `.`,
`./browser`, `./server`, and `./styles`. `emitEvent` and `bindEventMap`
live in `helpers.ts`, the `isButtonHost` and `isButtonEvent` guards in
`validators.ts`, and `AppError` with `isAppError` in `src/core/errors.ts`.
The guide carries one Surface row per new export and the method tables,
the parity minimum.

The proofs read both hosts through the installed helpers on managed
Chromium and Edge: the ordered mutations through a `MutationObserver` on
a button and on an anchor, the event's type and flags, hook release,
exact restoration, a detached host, destruction during a pending toggle,
concurrent instances, the delegated click on a child element, engine
reuse across clicks on a fragment root, a host inserted later, the three
disabled forms, a consumer-owned host and nested roots without a listener
error, a host moved out of the root pruned and reacquired, and the single
root listener with its release. Planted controls reddened and were
restored; every gate exits 0. The unit ran under briefs 1 to 6, audited in
rounds 1 to 3, which scaffold records in its
`.orkestrel/veneer/u7b-audit-verdict.md` file.
