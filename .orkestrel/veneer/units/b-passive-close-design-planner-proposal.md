# B-PASSIVE-CLOSE design, subjective lane (`planner` on Opus 5.5) — proposal

Brief: `units/b-passive-close-design-brief.md`. Retained as returned.

Lane held: **subjective** (`planner` on Opus 5.5, native, clean context). I edited nothing and ran nothing.

The design has one idea. Several places in the tree keep a list that every family landing has to extend, and each has gone stale. Each unit replaces one of those lists with a rule that has one home, a proof that pins the rule, and at most one source the tree already derives. That covers the showcase paragraph, the neighbour sentences, the per-family `*_KEYS` lists, the stem table, and the copies of the motion literal.
