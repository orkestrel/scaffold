Leave the select caret and the switch knob to their own dark rules

B-FORMS-ASSETS (`builder` on Sonnet, D26) removes `select-indicator` and `switch-knob` from the
`$assets` map, so the dark theme scope no longer declares `--bs-form-select-bg-img` and
`--bs-form-switch-bg`: the select and check partials declare each on the release's own dark
component rule, and a light island nested inside a dark one keeps the dark caret and knob as the
release does. `COMPONENT_DARK_ASSETS` names the two variables for the theme and tokens proofs, the
select proof's dark-caret case and the guide's three sentences follow, and a plant that re-adds an
entry reddens the theme case.
