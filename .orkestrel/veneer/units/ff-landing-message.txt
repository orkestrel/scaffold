Frame each focus ring on its lifted specimen and prove the keyboard outline paints (FOCUS-FRAME)

Every converted focus scenario is an element frame of its specimen lifted into a padded wrapper, reached by Tab, with
a positive ring reach and no cropped ring or region edge; the arrival frame is the one page frame. The skip-link and
list-group focus cases read the auto outline from pixels in the strip it paints, against a suppressed-outline control,
so a scripted focus that matches :focus-visible and paints nothing fails them. The ring-reach tables live in frozen
setup constants, and every branch of computeRingReach has a row that decides it. The accordion case watches the parked
pointer, and the grouped radio copies drop their names so the specimen keeps its checked control.
