Ship Button's cascade with its deferral partition and shipped rows (U7a)

`src/styles/elements/_button.scss` gives a bare `<button>` Elements'
compact neutral default in the `elements` layer: 6px block and 12px
inline padding, no border, a 6px radius, hover and active tints mixed
from the state mixer at 12% and 22%, the focus ring through the
`focus-ring` mixin, the disabled opacity, and the feedback transition
with its reduced-motion pair. `src/styles/components/_button.scss` owns
`.btn`, every `--bs-btn-*` custom property Bootstrap declares bound to a
`--vn-*` token or a `color-mix()` over one, the filled and outline
variants per role with tertiary added as a departure, the sizes and their
group-size mates, the link treatment, the `.btn-check` label states, the
disabled hosts, and forced-colours fallbacks. The `light` role's text is
the dark foreground Bootstrap gives it, so it reads over its fill. The
registry gains the state mixer, the Button defaults, and the neutral
focus highlight and reset tokens; `theme-tokens` closes the mixer per
mode; `focus-ring` takes its highlight and reset as parameters.

`guides/veneer.md` § Compatibility flips Button's `selector` and
`variable` rows to `shipped` and `tests/conformance.test.ts` lists `btn`,
so the row-keyed presence check now fires: every official `.btn` selector
and `--bs-btn-*` property is present in the built cascade or listed
under § Styles `### Deferred selectors` with its owner, and no deferred
name is present. The U3-retained `--bs-btn-close-filter` stays declared
and appears in no deferral row. The proofs read every state on managed
Chromium and Edge through the installed helpers against the run-6
calibration strings, including the outline roles' held active paint and
focus ring, the `light` role's contrast floor, the other roles' measured
ratios pinned, an unchecked `.btn-check` label refusing the active paint,
and the disabled shadow read through the compatible property. The
forced-colours reading stays an open row until the installed media stage
gains that axis. The calibrated dark fills read below 4.5:1 under white
text (dark primary at 2.59:1); they are Elements' measured values and are
recorded as a design question.

The physical-axis guard admits a symmetric shorthand: the build merges
logical border declarations into `border: 0`, whose CSSOM expansion the
guard refused, so `filterAsymmetricDeclarations` drops a physical longhand
whose opposite-side twin carries the same value in the same rule and the
guard reads each rule through it. The case matrices the Button proofs
read live in `tests/setupStyles.ts` as exported tables.

Six planted controls reddened and were restored byte for byte; every
gate exits 0 on managed Chromium and Edge. The unit ran as ten briefs
across two audit rounds; the stops were the Orchestrator's scope
omissions and two findings refuted by measurement, each recorded in the
campaign's re-baseline record.
