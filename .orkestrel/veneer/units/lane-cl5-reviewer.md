The recorded closure path — one successor owning `_heading.scss`, `_type.scss`, and `_mixins.scss` — is correct. Not this unit's to fix.

**B — the three specimen sections have one body: REAL, and this unit created two of the three copies.** `app/browser/sections/ContentSection.ts:13-45`, `TypeSection.ts:13-45`, and `MediaSection.ts:13-45` are identical apart from the two constants each reads. `AGENTS.md` § TTTDD step 3 routes repeated behaviour through one shared implementation. The distinction it drew — take an unlisted edit that a red gate forces, decline an unlisted refactor that nothing forces — is defensible, and the finding names the exact owner set a successor needs. Ruled a successor brief, not a defect of this round. On the dispatch's design question: the two new sections are shaped as a reader would expect, because they mirror the existing one exactly and the medium cut (text-flow classes against image and figure classes) matches the region names and the guide sentence. The one soft spot is that `TypeSection` also carries the list and quotation classes, which its own name does not suggest; the region copy at `app/browser/constants.ts:39-40` and `guides/veneer.md:838-840` both disclose it, so it stands.

**C — `.mark` and `.figure-caption` disagree with their tags: REAL, and understated.** `src/styles/components/_type.scss:43-47` pads on all sides and paints from the highlight aliases while `src/styles/elements/_mark.scss:3` pads on the inline axis only and paints nothing; `_image.scss:24-26` reads `--bs-secondary-color` (`_mixins.scss:242`) while `figcaption` reads `--vn-text-muted` through `caption-text` (`_mixins.scss:22-26`). Both are pinned by deliberate assertions (`type.test.ts:88-89`, `image.test.ts:65`). The finding is right that the departure is on the tag side and that brief 2 correction 7 directed the class side. Two things it misses: `.figure` is a third such pair (finding 13), and Bootstrap builds `.mark` and `.small` the same way it builds `.h1` — `@extend mark`, `@extend small` at `node_modules/bootstrap/scss/_type.scss:48-54` — so the twin reasoning that produced the heading ruling applies to `.mark` word for word and was not applied. `.small` is harmless because `87.5%` and `0.875em` resolve alike; `.mark` produces exactly the on-page disagreement the heading ruling exists to prevent, and `src/styles/elements/_mark.scss` being off-limits is a genuine bar.

**D — the departures table sits under § Tokens and carries selector rows: REAL but not this unit's.** The mismatch predates it — `figure`, `img`, `table`, `td`, and `legend` are already selector rows under a `Token` heading at `guides/veneer.md:678-687`. It is a guide-structure decision and out of this audit's stated scope.

None of the four is an invented finding shielding the unit from a lane's own; A, B, and C are real and C is the one the unit understated.
