Ship the background, border, and rounded keys with their sections and proofs

The background, border, and rounded keys ship in the utilities layer through the utility and
utility-variable mixins, every property declaration important as the release writes it: the fills walk
the aliased roles and their subtle and emphasis tiers, the opacity locals sit at the empty infix, the
border widths read the width steps, and the rounded entries read the radius tokens beside the border
entries. The Background and Border regions render captioned swatches through one shared helper, each
swatch that would match the page drawn with a border or a fill that shows it. The Tailwind profiles
proof compares each profile's whole layer order, the case tables live in the setup file, frozen and
bound by derivation, each named mutation turns its case red, and the guide carries both sections, their
compatibility rows, and the paint names off the Tailwind exclusion line.
