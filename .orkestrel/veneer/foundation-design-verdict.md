## Findings carried from the fix units into the chunks

- **Registry population (foundation-fix-3).** Bootstrap declares `--bs-breakpoint-xs` through `--bs-breakpoint-xxl` in a second `:root` block (`bootstrap.css:782-789`), outside the 117-name `:root, [data-bs-theme=light]` block the registry pins. - **Lift order inside one block (foundation-fix-6).** Sass emits a selector's normal declarations together and the lifted important ones after them, so a normal declaration authored after an `unlayer` call on the same selector moves ahead of it in the output. Bootstrap interleaves the two in a few blocks (the `.navbar-expand-* .offcanvas` reset, for one). The CSSOM equality of link 3 therefore compares a merged rule's declarations as two stable sequences, normal and important, rather than one interleaved sequence; a duplicate of one property with mixed importance is the one case that would need a per-case ruling under the "unless pivotal" clause.
- **Real Tailwind's `properties` layer (absorb-tailwind rows T8, C3).** Tailwind prepends `@layer properties;` to its compiled output for the `@property` fallback of engines without `@property`. That layer must stay first, so the full order statement goes inside the consumer's Tailwind entry before `@import 'tailwindcss'`; a separate Veneer sheet linked ahead of a separately compiled Tailwind sheet appends `properties` last. The guide states the recipe that way; the Tailwind chunk proves it beside the exclusion recipe.
- **Chromium face projects (foundation-fix-5).** 120 full-sheet files: 13.48 s isolated, 5.85 s with `isolate: false`, 1.48 s in the Node threads pool. The face projects adopt `isolate: false` (one module instance across files; Vite transforms each sheet once in either setting); text proofs stay in Node.
