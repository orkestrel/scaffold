# Tailwind flip: design brief

The user's ruling of 2026-10-04 inverts the authority of the Tailwind compatibility layer. This brief states the ruling, what stands today with its evidence, the constraints that stay, and the questions the design round must answer. `measurements.md` beside this file holds the readings the measurement lane takes before the planners design; `design-verdict.md` records the round's outcome.

## The user's ruling (2026-10-04)

Quoted from the user's message to the cloud session, lightly trimmed:

> Since we should now have the styles of bootstrap and tailwindcss deterministically mapped, I want to turn things on its head. I still want tailwindcss to be the compatibility layer, we are not providing the full tailwindcss the way we do for bootstrap, but now instead of tailwindcss bending to bootstrap at conflict, I want the compatibility to make bootstrap bend to tailwindcss and for us to properly curate that so it doesn't just break bootstrap, especially the components since the idea is tailwindcss to remain a utility library and to create this compatibility layer so that tailwindcss can use bootstrap as its missing component library. This is also why I wanted showcase and journey to have both bootstrap and tailwindcss so that we can toggle between them and especially now be able to see the difference between them with and without the tailwindcss layer. In other words, we are flipping the tailwindcss compatibility layer on its head and switching the authority and adding compatibility so that now bootstrap components are tuned to tailwindcss.

Read as four rulings:

- R1. At every conflict between Bootstrap and real Tailwind, Tailwind wins. A conflict is a shared class name (both declare it) or a bare-element declaration both resets make (Tailwind's preflight against Bootstrap's reboot).
- R2. Tailwind stays the utility library the consumer supplies. Veneer ships no Tailwind, recreates no Tailwind rule, and exports no Tailwind map.
- R3. The compatibility layer curates the flip so Bootstrap's components keep working: Bootstrap is the component library a Tailwind page is missing. A component that the flip breaks is repaired by the layer, never by taking the conflict back from Tailwind globally.
- R4. The showcase and its journeys show the difference: the faces toggle between Bootstrap alone and Bootstrap with Tailwind, with and without the compatibility layer, and the resolved-style readings prove what each face computes.

The user's word overrides the earlier rulings these reverse: `foundation-design-verdict.md` ruling 1 ("Bootstrap wins on a shared class", bound to the Tailwind layer alone), ruling 2's clause "`bootstrap` follows `base` so reboot beats preflight", and its refusal of "folding layered Bootstrap into `./tailwindcss`". `plan.md` § Standing rulings and `ROADMAP.md` carry the same sentences; the landing unit amends them.

## What stands today

Every fact names the file that established it; check a fact against the tree, not against this brief.

### Surfaces and cascade

- Order statement, first rule of every built sheet: `@layer reset, base, bootstrap, theme, elements, components, surfaces, composables, modifiers, utilities;` (`veneer/src/bootstrap/_tokens.scss:9`, `veneer/src/tailwindcss/_tokens.scss:2`).
- `./bootstrap` is the lifted recreation of Bootstrap 5.3.8: every normal declaration inside `@layer bootstrap`, every `!important` declaration outside every layer through the `unlayer` mixin (`veneer/src/bootstrap/_mixins.scss:8-16`, `:66-86`). The drop-in form (`$layered: false`) is byte-equal to `bootstrap.css` after one Sass round trip (guide § Bootstrap sheet, `veneer/guides/veneer.md:967-1147`). The reboot lives in `veneer/src/bootstrap/_reset.scss` (385 lines) inside the `bootstrap` layer and lifts two `display: none !important` declarations (`:284` the datalist picker indicator, `:383` `[hidden]`). The utilities are generated from the `$utilities` map by the `utility` mixin (`veneer/src/bootstrap/_utilities.scss:6`, `:1043-1075`), every value `!important` and therefore unlayered.
- `./tailwindcss` ships three things and nothing else: the order statement, one `@source not inline(...)` statement naming all 2025 `CLASS_NAMES.bootstrap` names (`veneer/src/tailwindcss/_tokens.scss:3`), and the preflight mirror in `@layer base`, every normal preflight declaration with its value replaced by `revert-layer` (`veneer/src/tailwindcss/_reset.scss`, 186 lines), with the `[hidden]` important rule exempted (guide § Tailwind compatibility sheet, `veneer/guides/veneer.md:1197-1321`). The sheet declares no class name (`veneer/tests/src/tailwindcss/index.test.ts:40`).
- The recipe a consumer writes is three lines: the order statement, `@import 'tailwindcss'`, `@import '@orkestrel/veneer/tailwindcss'` (`veneer/tests/setupServer.ts:945`). Tailwind's `@layer properties;` prelude must stay first; the literal statement follows it (guide § Load real Tailwind, `:1452-1519`). Linking a separate Veneer sheet ahead of the compiled Tailwind sheet misorders `properties`.
- Cascade facts the guide pins: an unlayered `!important` beats every layered normal declaration whatever the load order; a layered `!important` outranks every unlayered `!important`; an unlayered normal rule beats every layered rule; `revert-layer` in `base` rolls back to the layers before `base` and keeps a presentational hint, where `revert` reaches the user-agent sheet (`:1147-1197`, `:1270-1300`).

## Constraints that stay

- R2 as stated: `tailwindcss` 4.3.3 stays a `devDependency` imported only from tests and setup; the map stays internal; `src/core` holds no Tailwind group.
- `./bootstrap` stays the drop-in recreation: link 1, link 2, and link 3 hold unchanged, and a Bootstrap-only consumer sees no change. A planner who needs a change to the Bootstrap face states it as a bounded, switch-driven derivation from the same authored source, the way the lift is.
- Chromium only. Every claim measured and pinned; every record bound by digest; derived relationships derived in Chromium and never stored unless the design says why.
- The scaffold rules bind every file: `.claude/rules/styles.md` (one emitter home in `_mixins.scss`, the order statement once per sheet, no foreign layer, instruments in `tests/setupStyles.ts`), the writing rules, `AGENTS.md`.
- One recipe for the consumer, with the `properties` placement contract kept.
- The showcase shows every `CLASS_NAMES.bootstrap` name under every face; the faces differ only in the `style` elements the document holds; the authored page carries no `style` attribute at rest; the journeys drive the face through the header buttons; the resolved-style matrix proves what each face computes; the page uses no `dark:` utility.
- Do not over-optimize: substantive proofs, no superfluous mechanism, no chase for the last seconds.

## Questions the round answers

- Q1. Shared utility names (192). Bootstrap's rule for each is an unlayered `!important` declaration, which no layered normal Tailwind rule can beat. How does Bootstrap yield? Candidates: (a) a Tailwind-tuned Bootstrap build that withholds those names' rules, derived from the same `$utilities` map by a switch; (b) a layered `!important revert-layer` counter in the compatibility sheet (its rollback target is measurement M1); (c) emitting those names as layered normal declarations in `./bootstrap` itself, which changes a Bootstrap-only consumer's semantics and is refused unless pivotal; (d) another mechanism. Rule on each.
- Q2. Shared component names (17). Does Bootstrap keep them, Tailwind withholding its utility through the exclusion statement, because the name is a component contract (`collapse` is engine-written, `container` and `col-*` carry the grid, `table` the table component)? What does `caption-top` do, where both rules agree?
- Q3. The exclusion statement after the flip. Today it names all 2025 Bootstrap names. Candidates: name every Bootstrap name except the 192 shared utilities, so Tailwind generates exactly the names both ship by default and a consumer `@theme` token never makes Tailwind generate another Bootstrap name (`bg-primary` under `--color-primary`); or name only the 17 components; or name nothing. Rule, and state the consumer-theme limit the ruling implies.
- Q4. Preflight authority. The mirror goes. Where does Bootstrap's reboot land so preflight wins where both declare while the reboot still supplies what preflight never declares (body color and background, link color, heading and paragraph margins are preflight's `margin: 0`, heading sizes are preflight's `inherit`)? Candidates: the reboot emitted into the `reset` layer (before `base`) in the Tailwind-tuned build; the reboot dropped; the reboot kept and `base` moved (refused: the order statement is shared). State what the two important reboot declarations do under each candidate.
- Q5. The curation rule. Define a broken component measurably. Candidates: (i) an element carrying a Bootstrap component class reads under the tuned composition what it reads under Bootstrap alone, and the layer repairs each departure on the class (`.modal-title` gains the heading size it took from `h5`), so a bare element without a Bootstrap class is Tailwind's and is expected to depart; (ii) Bootstrap's reboot restored inside every component root, so a consumer's bare `p` inside a card gets Bootstrap margins; (iii) functional repairs only (visibility, display, hit area), leaving typography to Tailwind. Measurement M3 lists the departures per component. The curation record is committed and pinned both ways against the sheet and the measurement, in the shape of the exemption table.
- Q6. Surface shape. What `./tailwindcss` ships after the flip, and whether the consumer still links `./bootstrap`. Candidates: `./tailwindcss` becomes "Bootstrap for Tailwind" (the tuned build plus the exclusion plus the curation rules, so the three-line recipe is complete); or `./tailwindcss` stays thin and a `./bootstrap/tailwind` export carries the tuned build; or the tuned build is a `$tailwind: true` switch on the `./bootstrap/scss` barrel. Name the exports, the SCSS barrels, the Vite wrappers, the `files` list, and the derivation proof that ties the tuned build to the lifted build (the lifted sheet minus the withheld rules, with the reboot moved, plus the curation rules, and nothing else).
- Q7. The showcase. Two faces or three (`Bootstrap only`, `Bootstrap with Tailwind` without the layer, `Bootstrap with Tailwind` with the layer)? The face statechart rows; `TAILWIND_READINGS` inverted (`mt-3` reads 12 px, `border-1` draws); the invariance proof becomes a departure-partition proof: under the tuned face every departure from Bootstrap alone sits on an element carrying a shared utility name, or on a bare element under preflight, and none sits on an element carrying a component class; the `unexcluded` compile becomes a face rather than a control. The Bootstrap with Tailwind section's specimens and captions. The guide's § Faces table.
- Q8. Records and proofs. Which records keep their meaning (`oracle.min.json`, `comparison.json`, `similar.json`), which change meaning (`incompatible.json` becomes the record of what flips; `preflight.json` becomes the record of the baseline Tailwind sets), which are new (the curation record, the tuned compiles in `recipe.json`), and which cases go, invert, or are added, placed in Node (`conformance`) or Chromium (`src:tailwindcss`, `integration`, the journeys). State how the preflight rows read on a host whose Chromium enumerates a different longhand set, so the proof lands from either host.
- Q9. Guide and roadmap. The sections to rewrite, the sentences to reverse, and the tenet wording for `ROADMAP.md` and `plan.md`.
- Q10. Units. Bounded units with owned files, cheap-first acceptance criteria, lane (objective to Astra or a builder, subjective to Opus), and order, one writer per checkout, with the showcase rebuild and the gates before landing.

## R11: Tailwind's tokens win too (the user, 2026-10-04)

The user's words: "make sure tailwindcss wins out but make sure we're not just doing the easy 1:1 but also where it counts like colors and scales, etc for example primary in bootstrap is blue, just because tailwindcss doesn't have something that says primary doesn't mean we shouldn't forget to update primary with the tailwindcss blue."

Ruling R11: under the layer, Bootstrap's design tokens take Tailwind's values where Tailwind has a counterpart: the theme colors (`primary` to Tailwind's blue, `secondary` to its gray, `success` green, `danger` red, `warning` amber or yellow, `info` cyan or sky, `light` and `dark` to the gray ends), the grays, the derived tints, shades, and color-mode variants, the body and border colors, the border radii, the font stacks, the type scale, the shadows, the focus ring, and the breakpoints and container widths where the two scales can agree, each mapped deterministically from the installed Tailwind theme and pinned by the Tailwind inventory digest. The map is a build input of the tuned face, not a consumer step; `./bootstrap` stays byte-identical. The proofs' component baseline becomes the tuned sheet alone (Bootstrap for Tailwind without Tailwind's compile), and a derivation proof pins the tuned sheet against the lifted sheet as token substitution plus the existing withholding, moving, copying, restoring, and scoping. The design round (`tailwind-flip/tokens/`) decides the map, the mechanism (a Sass parameter or a build-time substitution over the literal regions), and the proofs; it opens while U6 finishes and lands after the structural flip or with it, as the round rules.
