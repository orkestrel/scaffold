## Ruling

The user's words (2026-10-04): "make sure tailwindcss wins out but make sure we're not just doing the easy 1:1 but also where it counts like colors and scales, etc for example primary in bootstrap is blue, just because tailwindcss doesn't have something that says primary doesn't mean we shouldn't forget to update primary with the tailwindcss blue."

## Questions

1. **The map's policy.** Nearest color by ΔE, or role-by-ramp (Bootstrap's 100 to 900 to Tailwind's 100 to 900 in one family per hue, with the base at 500 or 600), or Bootstrap's algebra over Tailwind bases (swap the ten base colors and the grays, recompute every tint, shade, and mix with Bootstrap's own functions)? Which Tailwind family for each Bootstrap hue (`$blue`, `$indigo`, `$purple`, `$pink`, `$red`, `$orange`, `$yellow`, `$green`, `$teal`, `$cyan`; the grays to `gray`, `slate`, `zinc`, or `neutral`)? Which shade is the base, given that white text on Tailwind's blue-500 (L 62.3) reads below 4.5:1 and on blue-600 (L 54.6) above it, and that Bootstrap's components pair white text with every theme color's base (`.btn-primary`, `.badge.text-bg-primary`, `.bg-primary` with `.text-white`), while `$warning` and `$info` pair dark text? State the contrast rule (no pairing Bootstrap ships reads worse than Bootstrap alone; or every pairing reads 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and UI).
2. **Derived values.** Hover and active states (15/20/25), focus borders (tint 50), range thumbs (tint 70), table variants (shift −80 and the factors), link hover (shade 20), the dark-mode roles: recomputed with Bootstrap's algebra from the new bases, or mapped to Tailwind ramp neighbors (hover = the next darker step)? Alpha forms (`rgba(13, 110, 253, 0.25)`, the `-rgb` triplets) follow from the resolved sRGB of the chosen base.
3. **Which scales.** Radii by role (`--bs-border-radius-xl` 1rem to `--radius-xl` 0.75rem, or keep equal values only); font stacks (`--bs-body-font-family` to `--font-sans`'s literal stack; `--bs-font-monospace` to `--font-mono`); the type scale (h1 to h6, `.display-*`, `.lead`, `.small`, `.fs-*` to `--text-*` by role, with Tailwind's rem line heights or Bootstrap's unitless 1.2; keep or drop the fluid `calc()` forms); shadows (`--bs-box-shadow*` to `--shadow-*` by role); the focus ring (Bootstrap's algebra on the new primary, or Tailwind's ring convention); breakpoints and containers (align `sm/lg/xl/xxl` to 640/1024/1280/1536 so `md:` and `-md-` and every other infix agree, with the container max-widths following Tailwind's `max-width = breakpoint` or Bootstrap's `breakpoint − 36`; or keep Bootstrap's); spacing inside components (`.btn`, `.card`, `.form-control`, `.modal` paddings on Bootstrap's spacer, or Tailwind's `--spacing` multiples); transitions and z-indices (keep).
4. **The mechanism.** A `$palette` (and `$scale`) switch in the Bootstrap partials, with every literal in the 56 Sass files replaced by a function call that reads the map and returns Bootstrap's own value by default (byte-identical `./bootstrap`; a generated edit over 616 occurrences; SVG data URIs through Sass interpolation of the escaped color), configured through `src/tailwindcss/_tokens.scss` like the five switches; or a build-time text substitution over the compiled lifted CSS in `configs/src/vite.tailwindcss.config.ts` (a plugin with the same map; the `%23` forms and triplets handled; no Sass edit); or a hybrid (Sass for the `:root` blocks' 150 literals, substitution for the components). Judge by the law (the derived build configures the partials through switches the guide records), by determinism and provability (the derivation proof pins the substitution step), by the cost of the edit, and by the consumer's story.
5. **Output form.** Resolved sRGB hex everywhere (Bootstrap's form; needed for the `-rgb` triplets and the mixes), or Tailwind's `oklch()` literals where no mix is needed; gamut handling for oklch values outside sRGB.
6. **The proofs.** The derivation proof gains the substitution step (the tuned sheet equals the lifted sheet after substitution, withholding, moving, copying, restoring, scoping, and nothing else); a token proof (every literal of the lifted sheet maps through the map or is listed as kept, two-way, with a planted literal control); a contrast proof over Bootstrap's shipped pairings under the tuned sheet alone against Bootstrap alone; the curated witness case, the partition case, and the paired engine states case read their component baseline from the tuned sheet alone (adopted without Tailwind's compile) instead of Bootstrap alone, so the layer's preservation claim becomes "the recipe preserves Bootstrap for Tailwind" while a separate case pins "Bootstrap for Tailwind differs from Bootstrap by the map"; the records regenerate; the showcase readings and captions change where tokens change (the `Bootstrap only` face keeps Bootstrap's values; the two Tailwind faces show the map); the guide's consumer table and a token table.
7. **The 17 shared component names** under the layer with the map: `.container` max-widths, `.table` colors, `.collapse` unchanged; what a Tailwind user expects of `.container` under the layer when breakpoints align.
8. **Dark mode.** The `[data-bs-theme=dark]` roles from the Tailwind ramp (text-emphasis 300, bg-subtle 900 or 950, border-subtle 700) or from Bootstrap's algebra over the new bases; the body colors in dark mode (`#212529` body bg to gray-900 or gray-950; `#dee2e6` body color to gray-200).
9. **Order and landing.** The token units follow the structural flip's landing or join it; the showcase's existing contrast cases must pass under the map; the budget of the journey is not widened by the map.
