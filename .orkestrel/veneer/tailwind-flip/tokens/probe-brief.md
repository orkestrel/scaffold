## Outputs under `/home/user/veneer/tmp/probes/tokens/`

1. `inventory.json`: every distinct color literal in the lifted sheet (hex in any form, `rgb()`/`rgba()`, `-rgb` triplets inside custom properties, colors inside `url("data:image/svg+xml,...")` as `%23RRGGBB` escapes or `fill='...'`/`stroke='...'`), with: the normalized sRGB hex, the occurrence count, the contexts (an array of `{ selector, property, layer, kind }` where `kind` is one of `custom-property` (inside `:root`, `[data-bs-theme=...]`, or a component's `--bs-*` declaration), `component-literal` (a plain declaration inside a component rule), `utility` (inside a withheld or kept utility rule), `data-uri`), the Sass origin when derivable (`{ base: '$blue', function: 'tint-color', weight: 80 }` or `{ variable: '$gray-600' }` or `unknown`), and the color-mode (`light`, `dark`, or `both`).
2. `tailwind-theme.json`: every variable `theme.css` declares, parsed: name, raw value, and for colors the oklch triple and the converted sRGB hex (convert yourself with the standard oklch to sRGB matrices; state the matrices and the gamut handling you used, and check against a known pair such as `--color-white: #fff` and `--color-black: #000`).
3. `nearest.json`: for every Bootstrap literal in `inventory.json`, the three nearest Tailwind colors by ΔE in oklch (Euclidean over L, a, b computed from oklch, with the hue difference weighted as oklab does), each with its name, hex, and distance; and separately the nearest within the same hue family where a family is obvious (the blues for `$blue` and its tints and shades, the grays for `$gray-*`), so the design round can prefer a consistent family over the raw nearest.
4. `scales.json`: side by side, Bootstrap's and Tailwind's values for radii (`--bs-border-radius*` against `--radius-*`), font stacks (`--bs-font-sans-serif`/`--bs-body-font-family` against `--font-sans`; `--bs-font-monospace` against `--font-mono`), the type scale (Bootstrap's `h1` to `h6` resting sizes and their fluid `calc()` forms, `.display-1` to `.display-6`, `.lead`, `.small`, `.fs-1` to `.fs-6` against `--text-*` with line heights), font weights (`--bs-body-font-weight`, heading weight 500, `.fw-*` against `--font-weight-*`), line heights, shadows (`--bs-box-shadow*` against `--shadow-*`), the focus ring (`--bs-focus-ring-*` against Tailwind's ring defaults in `theme.css` or `utilities.css`), breakpoints (`576/768/992/1200/1400` and the `.container*` max-widths `540/720/960/1140/1320` against `--breakpoint-*` and `--container-*`), the spacing scale (`$spacer` multiples `0.25/0.5/1/1.5/3rem` against `--spacing` multiples), transitions (`0.15s ease-in-out` against `--default-transition-*` and `--ease-*`), and z-indices (Bootstrap's against none). For each row: both raw values, equal or not, and the occurrence count of the Bootstrap literal in the lifted sheet.
5. `report.md`: the counts (distinct literals, occurrences by kind, by color-mode, by file in `src/bootstrap/`), the checks you ran (the tint and shade assertions, the oklch conversion check), the open inputs (a literal with no Sass origin, a Tailwind variable with no Bootstrap counterpart or the reverse), the version strings, and the exact commands; plain prose, numbers with their source, no recommendation.
