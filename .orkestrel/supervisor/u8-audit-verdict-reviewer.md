## Findings outside the claims

**F1. `focus.css` records the wrong mechanism for the invariant it depends on.** `/workspace/supervisor/app/browser/styles/focus.css:10-13` says "those rules are a class beside the state, this is an element beside the state, the two weigh the same, and this stylesheet loads last." An element beside the state is (0,1,1) and `.btn:focus-visible` is (0,2,0); it would lose, load order notwithstanding, and every ring in the contrast table would vanish. The rule only ties because `:is()` takes the specificity of its most specific argument, and `[tabindex]` is an attribute selector at (0,1,0), lifting the compound to (0,2,0). The comment therefore presents `[tabindex]` as coverage when it is the load-bearing term: a maintainer who trims the list to the element names that are "already focusable" silently drops the ring on every framework-classed control, and only three contrast readings would notice. Fix: state that the `:is()` list's specificity comes from `[tabindex]`, that this ties `.btn:focus-visible` at (0,2,0), that source order then decides, and that removing `[tabindex]` breaks the tie.

**F2. Three controls keep the halo, and the instrument would certify them anyway.** The feed register filters (`app/browser/components/FeedList.vue:114-128`) are the one control family in the application whose visible half is addressed at (0,3,0) by `.btn-check:focus-visible+.btn` (`halfmoon.min.css:21`), so they retain `outline: 0` plus `var(--bs-btn-focus-box-shadow)` while the app ring lands on a `clip:rect(0,0,0,0)` input. No reading in `contrast.test.ts` covers them, and adding one naively would not find it: `readFocus` resolves the focused element, which is the clipped input, and would report the ring's full ratio for chrome no reader can see. Bounding it: every other framework rule that removes the outline — `.nav-link:focus-visible`, `.navbar-toggler:focus-visible`, `.form-select:focus`, `.form-check-input:focus`, `.btn-close:focus`, `.accordion-button:focus` — sits at (0,2,0) and loses to load order, so the ring genuinely reaches everything else, as the three measured rings show. Fix: add the `.btn-check:focus-visible + .btn` pair to `focus.css`, and add a reading that measures the label rather than the input.
